# B2 Porsche final report

28 September 2026. This reports **local implementation**, compared with the preserved pre-B2 working tree. B2 has not been committed, pushed or deployed. The full evidence register is in [keyword coverage](b2-porsche-keyword-coverage.csv), [ownership](b2-porsche-intent-ownership.csv), [before/after](b2-porsche-before-after.csv), [gap decisions](b2-porsche-gap-decisions.md), [technical sources](b2-porsche-technical-sources.md) and [regression report](b2-porsche-regression-report.md).

## 1. Executive Summary

**PASS — READY FOR REVIEW** for local B2 implementation. We reviewed 101 Porsche URLs, changed 34, and created none. The work clarified service, system, model, symptom and guide intent while preserving URL policy. All 487 normalized keyword terms have an editorial decision; 25 unsupported or unsuitable terms are intentionally not targeted. There are zero unresolved primary-owner conflicts and zero classified gaps. This is not a claim that rankings, CTR or leads improved.

## 2. Scope Completed

Porsche-only copy, metadata, contextual links, selected SSR FAQ visibility and lastmod updates were made. Twenty source/generated files differ from the pre-B2 checkpoint. No B3, BMW, Ferrari, Lamborghini or unrelated brand optimization was done.

## 3. Existing Porsche Architecture

The cluster has 101 Porsche-path URLs: 81 English and 20 Arabic. It already includes a broad hub, commercial services, models/generations, system explainers, problem guides, maintenance guides and the separate best-workshop page. Existing owners were preferred over additional URLs. The URL-by-URL architecture and indexability are in the ownership CSV.

## 4. Porsche Keyword Evidence

The workbook yielded 421 Porsche Master Keywords rows, 59 GSC Opportunities copies and eight Brand Specific Systems rows. Seven original GSC exports yielded 373 Porsche/system query observations with overlapping date windows. The latest six-month top-query subset has 60 rows, 2,925 impressions and two clicks; 89 distinct normalized terms appear across all original exports. These are query-only observations, not query × landing-page evidence. Forty-seven B2 brief examples are separate, unmeasured candidates.

## 5. Keyword Normalization

908 provenance-preserving observations reduced to 495 raw strings and 487 normalized terms. Punctuation, minor wording and duplicate source rows were consolidated for intent decisions while source, period and measured/generated labels were retained. Metrics are blank where unavailable; overlapping GSC windows were not summed.

## 6. Keyword → Owner Map

Each normalized term has a primary editorial owner, coverage class and reason in the coverage CSV. The ownership CSV assigns each indexable Porsche URL a distinct primary task. The map distinguishes measured queries from taxonomy and research suggestions; it does not assert Google's actual ranking URL.

## 7. Broad Porsche Hub Review

The hub retains broad Porsche repair/service discovery. Its service directory now exposes the 14 existing commercial service routes and a relevant interval guide. The hub does not claim ownership of every model, system or symptom query. Its English diagnostic language names PIWIS 3 with supported-function qualifiers; the Arabic hub's existing paths were linked directly.

## 8. Commercial Service Review

Porsche-specific service copy was sharpened for oil, brakes, transmission/PDK, suspension/PASM, AC, mechanical, battery, electrical/PCM and diagnostics. Inspection precedes component conclusions. Universal oil-grade, fluid and interval claims were removed. Service pages now link to relevant system, problem and guide owners where useful. Booking/contact intent remains clear, with no free-diagnostic promise.

## 9. PDK Review

The existing PDK page remains an educational system owner. Transmission repair owns commercial inspection/repair; shift-symptom guides own triage. B2 avoids treating every shift complaint as an internal gearbox failure or claiming a universal fluid schedule.

## 10. PASM Review

The PASM explainer, PASM-fault problem page and suspension service retain separate tasks: system understanding, warning interpretation and booking. PASM and air suspension are not treated as interchangeable or universal equipment.

## 11. Other Porsche Systems Review

Existing PDCC, PTM, PCCB, Tiptronic, air-suspension, rear-axle-steering and Sport Chrono content was retained with scope and fitment qualifiers. PCM, PSM, active mounts and turbo topics remain sections or contextual mentions where evidence does not justify another thin acronym page.

## 12. Model / Generation Review

Existing 911, 997, 991, 992, Cayenne, Macan, Panamera, 718, Boxster, Cayman and Taycan routes were reviewed. Six compact model pages no longer repeat generic three-part bullet templates. Taycan now uses EV-relevant charging/12V/chassis service context rather than oil, PDK or engine-warning prompts. No model × service URL was added and no universal equipment/failure claim was introduced.

## 13. Problem / Symptom Review

Existing problem guides remain symptom-led, with cause categories and diagnostic next steps rather than a promised part diagnosis. Problem-to-service relationships were checked; new thin symptom pages were not justified by generated phrases alone.

## 14. Guide Review

The service-interval guide is linked from the hub and relevant services. It interprets the applicable vehicle schedule instead of assigning one Porsche-wide interval. Other ownership and maintenance guides remain informational with contextual service routes.

## 15. Missing Commercial Service Decisions

Potential AC, cooling, turbo, body, tuning, paint-care, key-programming, retrofit and other service phrases were individually assessed in the gap-decision register. Existing service owners handle supported demand. Unsupported specific offerings are intentionally untargeted; no new commercial URL was needed.

## 16. FAQ Review

Porsche FAQs were made specific to service or model intent. Duplicate generic Arabic service questions were removed. A rendered audit initially found FAQ answers missing from initial HTML; Porsche-only accordion force-mounting fixed this. All 114 emitted FAQ question/answer pairs now have visible matching server-rendered text.

## 17. Internal Linking Review

The rendered audit checked 1,412 Porsche internal links: zero broken, redirecting or fragment-missing links. Contextual anchors connect hub → services and service ↔ system/problem/guide without adding artificial sitewide links. No nonexistent Arabic target was found.

## 18. Orphan / Crawl Depth Review

No Porsche route was orphaned in the rendered graph. Maximum crawl depth from a same-language entry point was three. Breadcrumbs and directories remain in place, including the Arabic hub's actual Arabic routes.

## 19. Schema Review

The cluster emits appropriate Service, Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage and other existing page types. No schema consistency issue was found; 114 FAQ pairs match visible answers. No Review, AggregateRating, price/Offer or manufacturer affiliation was fabricated.

## 20. Technical Claim Review

PIWIS 3 capability is attributed to the B2 business brief, but programming/coding is conditional on vehicle, function and control unit. PDK, PASM, PCCB and maintenance copy was checked against Porsche sources. Diagnosis is described as investigation, not certainty from a fault code. Applicable model/year/specification requirements govern intervals and equipment.

## 21. E-E-A-T / Trust Review

The independent Al Quoz workshop, existing contact paths and confirmed PIWIS 3 capability provide the legitimate business context. No Porsche authorization, technician certification, awards, ratings, customer counts, warranties or case studies were added without evidence.

## 22. Content Quality Review

Repetitive model template bullets and generic service wording were reduced. Each changed page is tied to a specific customer task and next inspection/service action. Shared navigation and contact components were retained; no substantive four-paragraph duplicate pair was found in the automated comparison.

## 23. Arabic Review

All 20 existing Arabic Porsche routes were included in architecture and policy checks. Existing Arabic hub and service relationships were improved where relevant. No fake language symmetry or Arabic problem/model route was created. Robots, canonical, hreflang and language-switch policy did not change; 242 rendered hreflang links passed checks.

## 24. Image / Alt Review

Rendered representative pages had no broken image or misleading model image in the tested set. Existing descriptive alt/decorative behavior was retained; no keyword-stuffed alt was introduced. The browser evidence covers representative routes rather than every image request on the live site.

## 25. CTA Review

Commercial pages lead to contact, booking or inspection; problems lead to diagnosis/inspection; system and guide pages keep an educational lead-in. No free or instant diagnosis is promised. Eight FAQ interactions and two navigation journeys passed local browser checks.

## 26. Local SEO Review

Dubai, Al Quoz and UAE context remains natural to the workshop and service task. Heat/driving references are used only where relevant. No neighborhood doorway or fictitious branch was created.

## 27. Canonical / Indexability Regression

Across 1,247 routes, there were zero route-policy and SEO-policy changes. Canonical, robots/noindex, redirect behavior and sitemap membership were preserved. `/best-porsche-workshop-dubai` was neither redirected, deleted nor merged. Thirty-four changed Porsche URLs received lastmod updates.

## 28. Cannibalization Regression

Broad hub, best-workshop comparison, model, guide, PDK/transmission/PDK symptom, PASM/PASM fault/suspension and diagnostics/problem owners have distinct primary tasks. The best-workshop overlap remains a future evidence-based consolidation question. No harmful cannibalization is claimed without dated query × page evidence.
