# B3 BMW final report

28 September 2026. This report evaluates the **local** B3 working tree against the preserved pre-B3 snapshot. Nothing was committed, pushed or deployed. Supporting registers: [keyword coverage](b3-bmw-keyword-coverage.csv), [intent ownership](b3-bmw-intent-ownership.csv), [before/after](b3-bmw-before-after.csv), [gap decisions](b3-bmw-gap-decisions.md), [technical sources](b3-bmw-technical-sources.md) and [regression](b3-bmw-regression-report.md).

## 1. Executive Summary

**PASS — READY FOR REVIEW** for local BMW implementation. Forty-five BMW-path URLs were reviewed, 21 rendered pages changed, and no URL was added. Existing hub, service, model, guide and Arabic owners were retained. All 490 normalized terms have an editorial placement or an explicit non-targeting reason; zero primary-owner conflicts remain. This does not assert a ranking or conversion gain.

## 2. Scope Completed

BMW-specific hub copy and navigation, commercial service descriptions and FAQs, workshop-selection titles, Arabic selection copy, and initial-HTML FAQ visibility were improved. Only 21 existing BMW routes received updated sitemap dates. No Mercedes, Porsche or unrelated brand output changed in meaning, SEO objects, links or image attributes.

## 3. Existing BMW Architecture

The route inventory contains 45 BMW-path URLs, including an English broad hub, 14 English commercial services, seven dedicated model/M-model pages, an English and Arabic best-workshop page, existing guides and the actual Arabic hub/services/guides. There is no BMW-only system or symptom route in the current architecture. Existing service sections were preferred over thin new URLs. The [ownership CSV](b3-bmw-intent-ownership.csv) records every URL's task, indexability and canonical.

## 4. BMW Keyword Evidence

The workbook supplied 409 BMW Master Keywords rows, 35 GSC Opportunities copies and eight Brand Specific Systems research rows. Seven original GSC files supplied 296 BMW/system query observations with overlapping date windows. The latest six-month top-query subset has 45 rows, 2,660 impressions and five clicks; 91 distinct normalized terms occur across original exports. Eighty-two explicit B3 brief examples were preserved as unmeasured candidates. Query exports lack query × landing-page joins.

## 5. Keyword Normalization

830 provenance-preserving observations yielded 537 raw strings and 490 normalized terms. Service/servicing, center/centre, gearbox/transmission and screen/display variants were consolidated only where the user task remained the same. ISTA+, iDrive, VANOS, Valvetronic, xDrive, ZF, battery registration, diagnosis, coding and programming remain distinct concepts. Overlapping GSC exports were not added together.

## 6. Keyword → Owner Map

The pre-edit [owner map](outputs/b3/bmw-keyword-owner-map.md) and [row-level records](outputs/b3/bmw-keyword-owner-records.csv) assign one existing editorial owner or intentional non-target decision to every observation. The map is an editorial plan, not proof of Google's ranking URL. It has zero unresolved primary-owner conflicts.

## 7. Broad BMW Hub Review

`/brands/bmw-service-dubai` remains the broad commercial discovery and service/model directory. It now states user-confirmed ISTA+ capability with vehicle/function limits, and adds six symptom-to-service pathways plus maintenance/M-guide links. Al Quoz, service scope and booking are clear without stuffing workshop variants.

## 8. Best BMW Workshop Review

The English and Arabic best-workshop pages now lead with **how to choose a BMW workshop**, preserving the selection criteria task. The broad hub retains service/repair/workshop/garage booking intent. `/best-bmw-workshop-dubai` was not redirected, deleted, merged or canonicalized to the hub. Any future consolidation requires query × page, backlink and conversion evidence.

## 9. Commercial Service Review

BMW oil, brakes, diagnostics, transmission, suspension, AC, mechanical, battery and electrical pages received more specific copy or FAQs. Existing body, exhaust, fuel, steering and tyre owners were reviewed and retained. Diagnosis comes before component or replacement claims, and quotes depend on the inspected scope.

## 10. Diagnostics / ISTA+ Review

The diagnostics service owns warning/fault investigation and supported coding enquiries. Copy distinguishes fault reading and physical testing from configuration coding and software programming. ISTA+ is a confirmed workshop capability in the B3 brief; available functions still depend on vehicle, control unit and access. A code is not described as a guaranteed failed-part identification.

## 11. Engine / Mechanical Review

The mechanical service owns engine, cooling, leak and turbo assessment. VANOS and Valvetronic remain generation-dependent sections rather than new acronym pages. Overheating and power-loss copy avoids remote part diagnosis; the relevant symptom leads to inspection or diagnostics first.

## 12. Transmission / ZF Review

The existing transmission service owns gearbox repair and supported maintenance enquiries. It already separated fluid service, control faults and internal repair. B3 retained that strong distinct page, while avoiding a claim that all BMWs use one ZF unit or that fluid service fixes every shift issue.

## 13. Suspension Review

The suspension page now distinguishes arms, bushes, dampers, tyres and geometry from optional air/adaptive equipment. A low corner or chassis warning does not by itself prove an air strut or compressor failure. Model and specification control fitment.

## 14. AC Review

The AC page now explains warm-air and weak-airflow inspection across refrigerant, condenser airflow, fans, blower, compressor and controls. It does not present a regas as the answer to every complaint. Refrigerant specification comes from the exact vehicle.

## 15. Electrical, iDrive and Screen Review

The electrical page is the existing commercial fault-assessment owner for iDrive, head-unit, screen and camera concerns. It distinguishes fault repair from an audio/infotainment upgrade. CCC/CIC/NBT references are conditional on fitted architecture; no separate thin unit page or universal replacement promise was added.

## 16. Battery Review

The 12V battery page now tests charging, connection and drain possibilities before recommending replacement. Battery specification, registration and any required coding remain vehicle-dependent. The copy explicitly separates 12V service from unverified high-voltage traction-battery repair.

## 17. Brakes, Oil and Maintenance Review

Brake copy identifies the fitted standard, M Sport, M Compound or ceramic hardware before parts are proposed. Oil copy uses engine/year-specific approval and Condition Based Service information instead of a universal grade or interval. The maintenance guide retains planning and cost-factor intent, separate from oil booking and the broad hub.

## 18. Cooling, Turbo and xDrive Review

Cooling and turbo phrases map to mechanical assessment; a power-loss symptom does not automatically identify the turbo. xDrive and transfer-case questions begin with diagnostics and can route to driveline repair after findings. xDrive is not treated as synonymous with the transmission.

## 19. Problem / Symptom Review

The hub now offers paths for drivetrain/engine warnings, shift changes, overheating/coolant loss, ride-height warnings, iDrive faults and battery warnings. Existing service pages provide diagnostic context and a booking step. The available exact measured evidence did not justify a separate BMW symptom URL; no thin warning-light page was created.

## 20. Model / M-Model Review

Existing 3 Series, 5 Series, X5, X6, M3, M4 and M5 pages were inspected. Their generation, engine, drivetrain and fitted-system distinctions provide useful model tasks, so most body copy was retained. FAQ answers on these pages are now present in initial HTML. M3/M4/M5 remain separate from standard 3/4/5 Series intent.

## 21. Electric BMW Review

No dedicated i4, i5, i7 or iX route exists in this architecture. No EV-only page was created from model-name keywords or populated with combustion-engine advice. The existing hub asks owners to confirm the model and requested scope; high-voltage repair capability was not invented.

## 22. Guide Review

The maintenance, BMW M and X5 guides retain informational/model support roles. The hub links to maintenance and M guides, with commercial services available as contextual next steps. Guides were not turned into broad BMW repair landing pages.

## 23. Missing Service and Page Decisions

The [gap register](b3-bmw-gap-decisions.md) records service, system, symptom, model and guide decisions. Exact retrofit, key, high-voltage and similar generated offerings were not asserted as available. No new URL passed the combined test of distinct user task, evidence and verified scope.

## 24. Title and Description Review

BMW service-page descriptions now distinguish their specific service rather than repeating the same generic sentence. The diagnostics title names ISTA+; the best-workshop titles in English and Arabic describe a selection task. Strong existing model and broad-hub titles were retained. The [before/after register](b3-bmw-before-after.csv) shows old/new values or `RETAINED`.

## 25. FAQ Review

FAQ questions were checked against the actual page task. Newly edited commercial pages answer practical scope and safety questions; the existing guide and model answers were retained. Initially 31 emitted FAQ answers were absent from initial HTML. BMW-scoped accordion rendering fixed this: all 167 emitted BMW FAQ pairs now match visible server-rendered answers.

## 26. Internal Linking Review

The BMW hub links to existing model, service, maintenance and symptom routes. The rendered audit checked 813 BMW internal links: zero broken, redirecting or fragment-missing targets. Navigation was not inflated with artificial sitewide links. A cross-brand shared navigation label was preserved so B2 Porsche output stayed unchanged.

## 27. Orphan and Crawl Depth Review

No BMW route was orphaned in the rendered internal graph. Maximum same-language depth from a locale entry point was four. The Arabic hub and existing Arabic guides/services remained discoverable without invented Arabic model or problem routes.

## 28. Technical Claim Review

ISTA+, ZF 8HP, xDrive, battery management, iDrive and Condition Based Service wording was checked against manufacturer/component-maker sources in the technical-source register. Equipment and functions are qualified by model, generation, specification, module and access. No universal interval, fluid, gearbox, air-suspension or failed-part claim was introduced.

## 29. E-E-A-T / Trust Review

The copy uses the existing independent Al Quoz workshop, contact paths and B3-confirmed ISTA+ capability. It does not invent BMW authorization, technician certification, ratings, awards, warranty, repair counts, case studies or prices. The established-year claim predates B3 and is not represented as years of BMW-specific experience.

## 30. Content Quality and Duplicate Review

Service copy now explains the driver concern, checks and next action rather than generic promotional language. Strong model-specific content was retained. The automated comparison found no pair with four or more identical long substantive paragraphs after excluding shared site chrome; this is a screen, not a substitute for editorial judgment.

## 31. Arabic Review

Existing Arabic BMW URLs were audited as actually implemented. The Arabic hub FAQs and maintenance/best-workshop FAQ answers are now available in initial HTML. The Arabic best-workshop title and lead were differentiated from broad repair booking. No Arabic path symmetry was invented and no robots, canonical, hreflang or language-switch policy changed; 128 BMW hreflang links passed validation.

## 32. Image / Alt Review

Representative desktop/mobile pages showed no broken visible image. Existing descriptive alt and decorative behavior were retained; no keyword-stuffed BMW alt was added. The browser sample does not constitute a live-site image crawl.

## 33. CTA and Local SEO Review

Commercial pages ask for booking or inspection details. Diagnostic and symptom paths avoid free or instant diagnosis claims. Guides link contextually toward a relevant service. Dubai and Al Quoz appear in appropriate location/service context without neighborhood doorway pages.

## 34. Schema Review

The BMW cluster uses existing Service, Article/BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage and page-type markup where rendered. Schema URLs and breadcrumbs passed the local audit; all 167 FAQ pairs match initial HTML. No fabricated Review, AggregateRating, Offer or manufacturer-affiliation node was added.

## 35. Canonical / Indexability and Cannibalization Regression

Across all 1,247 routes, there were zero route-policy and SEO-policy changes. The BMW hub, best-workshop page, model pages, services and guides have distinct editorial tasks. The best-workshop overlap remains a consolidation candidate, **not confirmed cannibalization**, because dated query × page, backlink and conversion evidence is missing.

## 36. Keyword Coverage, Remaining Gaps and CTR

Of 490 normalized terms: 269 primary, 172 section, nine model, seven informational-guide and 13 secondary coverage decisions; 20 are intentionally untargeted. Zero are classed `GAP — REVIEW REQUIRED`. The latest query-only GSC subset points to broad service, transmission, diagnostics/coding, engine, battery, AC and brakes as opportunities; the [CTR register](outputs/b3/bmw-ctr-opportunities.csv) preserves position and impression evidence without attributing a ranking URL or promising uplift.

Twenty representative routes were tested at desktop and mobile widths (40 checks), with eight FAQ interactions, two hub-to-diagnostics journeys and six no-JavaScript checks. The sampled pages had one H1, expected metadata/canonical/schema, no viewport overflow, no visible broken image, and no observed hydration/runtime error. All 45 BMW routes passed the automated initial-HTML checks.

## 38. Build and Prior-Batch Preservation

TypeScript, production build and existing SEO/routing/service validators passed. B0-A routing collections and 404 output remain unchanged; generated asset references refreshed normally. Mercedes and Porsche rendered SEO, visible content, links and images match their pre-B3 baselines. The separate [regression report](b3-bmw-regression-report.md) gives check counts and limits.

## 39. Remaining Risks and Future Opportunities

Future work may validate best-workshop consolidation, deeper drivetrain/iDrive guide value, model demand and any high-voltage or retrofit scope using real workshop and search evidence. No B4 work was begun.
