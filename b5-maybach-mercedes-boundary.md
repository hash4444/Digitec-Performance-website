# B5 Maybach / Mercedes search-ownership boundary

## 1. Why Maybach Needs Distinct Search Ownership
The Maybach badge and fitted rear-cabin, comfort and chassis equipment change the vehicle-specific enquiry. Broad Mercedes searches still belong to the B1 Mercedes cluster. Shared hardware alone does not justify merging their primary tasks.

## 2. Existing Maybach Architecture
The Maybach cluster has an English and Arabic hub, 14 commercial-service routes in each language, a best-workshop selection guide and an S580 owner guide in each language. There are no separate Maybach S-Class or GLS model routes.

## 3. Existing Mercedes Architecture
B1 has a Mercedes hub, dedicated commercial services, S-Class and GLS model resources, and system/maintenance content. B5 did not edit their substantive rendered content or SEO policy.

## 4. Broad Service Boundary
Maybach broad service: `/brands/maybach-service-dubai`. General Mercedes-Benz service: `/brands/mercedes-benz-service-dubai`. The Maybach hub now requests VIN and equipment details and points to Maybach owners; it links to Mercedes only for the wider family.

## 5. S-Class Boundary
The Maybach S580 guide answers Maybach-specific owner planning. The Mercedes S-Class guide serves the wider S-Class family. Neither is retargeted to own the other's badge-specific booking task.

## 6. GLS Boundary
Maybach GLS enquiries remain at the Maybach hub and its applicable services. The Mercedes GLS model resource remains a wider GLS owner. No thin Maybach GLS page was created.

## 7. Suspension / AIRMATIC Boundary
Maybach suspension: `/brands/maybach-service-dubai/suspension-repair`. Mercedes suspension: `/services/mercedes-suspension-repair-dubai`. AIRMATIC and optional E-ACTIVE BODY CONTROL must be identified by VIN, model and year. A ride-height symptom does not prove a failed strut.

## 8. Diagnostics / XENTRY Boundary
Maybach diagnostics: `/brands/maybach-service-dubai/engine-diagnostics`. Mercedes diagnostics retain their B1 owner. Supported module access and functions are confirmed for the vehicle; B5 does not extend a generic Mercedes tool claim into universal Maybach programming.

## 9. Transmission Boundary
Maybach transmission enquiries belong to `/brands/maybach-service-dubai/transmission-repair`; generic Mercedes gearbox enquiries remain with B1. A shift symptom calls for diagnosis before parts or replacement are proposed.

## 10. Electrical Boundary
Maybach rear-cabin and vehicle electrical concerns start at `/brands/maybach-service-dubai/electrical-repair`. B1 Mercedes electrical content remains the generic platform resource.

## 11. AC Boundary
Maybach cabin-zone and cooling enquiries: `/brands/maybach-service-dubai/ac-repair`; wider Mercedes AC searches retain B1 ownership. The fitted climate arrangement determines the inspection.

## 12. Battery Boundary
Maybach low-voltage battery enquiries: `/brands/maybach-service-dubai/battery-replacement`. Generic Mercedes battery advice remains B1. No traction-battery or high-voltage repair capability is asserted.

## 13. COMAND / MBUX Boundary
Maybach fitted display and rear-cabin concerns are covered as a section of Maybach electrical diagnostics. Mercedes COMAND/head-unit/MBUX information remains with B1. Hardware names depend on generation; no separate Maybach acronym page was made.

## 14. Maintenance Boundary
The Maybach S580 guide and hub support Maybach-specific planning. Mercedes maintenance guides retain the broader family. No universal interval, oil grade or package was introduced.

## 15. Internal-Link Strategy
Maybach hub → Maybach guide, suspension and diagnostics owners. Maybach → Mercedes links are contextual explanations for the wider family, while B1 model resources already link to Maybach for badge-specific scope. No sitewide artificial link was added.

## 16. Duplicate-Content Findings
Shared contact details, booking components and service-process facts are legitimate. The new Maybach hub section adds VIN, rear-cabin and fitted chassis context rather than copying B1 Mercedes body copy. Other inherited service templates remain a content-quality opportunity, not proven search cannibalization.

## 17. Ownership Conflicts
**Zero unresolved primary-intent conflicts in the editorial map.** No query × landing-page GSC export was supplied, so historical competition cannot be confirmed or excluded empirically.
