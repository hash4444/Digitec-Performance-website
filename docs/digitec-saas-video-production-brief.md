## Motion rules

- Keep transitions fluid and controlled: 350–600 ms ease-out for UI; 800–1200 ms camera moves.
- Use delicate technical lines, data pulses, circular scan rings, and restrained glass panels—never generic stock-dashboard visuals.
- Keep typography large, sparse, and left-aligned. Make the car and diagnostic animation the hero.
- Add a low cinematic engine ambience, soft electrical sweeps, confirmation ticks, and a restrained final impact. Avoid racing clichés or excessive revving.
