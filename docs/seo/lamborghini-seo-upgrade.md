## Technical corrections and official references

- Huracán EVO: seven-speed Lamborghini Dual Clutch (LDF), not an Urus transmission. Source: https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/huracan-evo
- Urus S: eight-speed automatic and adaptive air suspension. Sources: https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/urus-s and https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/gateway_urus/s/2024/brochure/URUS%20S_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_WCAG_EN_2024.pdf
- Aventador SVJ: ISR gearbox, not Huracán LDF. Source: https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/aventador-svj
- Removed universal transmission, suspension, battery chemistry, refrigerant, brake-size, oil-grade and oil-interval descriptions. Removed irrelevant inspection-test terminology from the Lamborghini diagnostic path through the page-specific override.
- FAQ content remains useful and visible; no FAQ rich-result promise is made. Google states FAQ rich results stopped appearing from 7 May 2026: https://developers.google.com/search/updates
