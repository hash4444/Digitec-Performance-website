## 6. Lead attribution and CRM integration specification

See the [full data model and reconciliation specification](../../outputs/ppf-phase2-2026-10-17/lead-attribution-crm-spec.md), [restricted received-lead design schema](../../outputs/ppf-phase2-2026-10-17/lead-model.schema.json) and [empty register header](../../outputs/ppf-phase2-2026-10-17/lead-register-template.csv).

The model covers lead ID, source/medium, campaign/ad group, known landing page, vehicle and coverage, qualification/quotation status and value, booking status and final invoiced value. Receipt, quote, booking and invoice evidence are separate source records. Values are nullable until known; unknown is not zero. The future intake maps the frontend unknown landing sentinel to null, validates controlled campaign pairings and uses decimal/minor-unit handling for money. Contract tests use a small floating-point tolerance for JSON-number validation; this is not a deployed accounting implementation.

The reference is not a lead ID or identity proof. Staff create a stable lead ID only after receiving the actual message, copy the reference/reported labels, deduplicate the real conversation, then link the actual quote, confirmed booking and final invoice. Direct WhatsApp/call enquiries or edited messages may remain unattributed. Current client UTM labels are reported context, not verified platform attribution. Missing data must stay unknown.

Existing `ppf_page_view`, `quote_started`, `whatsapp_click`, `whatsapp_draft_opened` and `telephone_link_clicked` remain. Direct-link `whatsapp_chat_opened` also remains. None proves a received enquiry. Do not sum these diagnostic events as separate leads or enable them as primary received-lead bidding goals. `quote_submitted` and `booking_confirmed` remain unimplemented until verified source evidence exists.

No receipt backend or approved CRM integration was found wired to this flow. The specification includes local synthetic intake tests, authentication, validation, access/retention, acknowledgement/outbox deduplication, privacy checks and account-approved import steps for a later authorized integration. Click IDs and raw/hashed customer identifiers are not captured for advertising imports here. Current Google documentation describes a June 2026 transition to Data Manager API with legacy-access caveats; the future account-supported method must be verified. No production database, messaging integration or webhook was created.
