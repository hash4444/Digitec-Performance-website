# G2 SAFETY URGENCY FRAMEWORK

## 1. Purpose

An editorial triage framework for content review, not remote diagnosis or permission to drive. Counts refer to conditional scenarios, never fixed urgency assigned to a keyword.

## 2. Safety Principles

Severity and associated symptoms govern the response. Book display/electrical assessment; do not operate controls while driving. Manufacturer examples in the technical-sources report are vehicle-specific. The scenario table below states the conservative editorial boundaries.

## 3. MONITOR / BOOK SERVICE

| Scenario | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Reduced cabin cooling only | Book AC inspection; avoid unsafe cabin heat or impaired demisting. |
| Touchscreen fault without safety-critical loss | Book display/electrical assessment; do not operate controls while driving. |
| Camera image absent | Do not rely on the failed camera; assess whether the manoeuvre can be completed safely and book inspection. |
| Soft-close convenience function inoperative | Confirm door can be safely latched; failed secure closure is a stronger safety concern. |


## 4. PROMPT INSPECTION

| Scenario | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Steady engine warning without other symptoms | Handbook and safe-travel assessment; not blanket driving permission. |
| Repeated battery discharge while safely parked | Electrical/charging assessment before replacement. |
| No-start in a safe location | Differentiate no crank, normal cranking and starts-then-stops; arrange assessment. |
| Mild intermittent shifting complaint | Transmission assessment; worsening behaviour or loss of drive escalates. |
| Suspension noise without control change | Inspect; unsafe handling or severe ride-height loss escalates. |
| Mild rough idle | Diagnostic assessment; severe running/flashing light escalates. |


## 5. LIMIT DRIVING

| Scenario | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Unexplained ongoing fluid loss | Determine severity and system; major loss/critical warning requires stopping. |
| New vibration without immediate loss of control | Avoid provoking the symptom; tyre/steering/brake context directs inspection. |
| New power loss | Assess safe travel; severe change/other warning requires assistance. |
| Unusual tyre pressure loss | Tyre/wheel/valve inspection; flat or compromised control means stop safely. |


## 6. STOP DRIVING / RECOVERY MAY BE APPROPRIATE

| Scenario | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Continuing overheating or steam | Stop safely, engine off; avoid hot pressurised system and seek assistance. |
| Oil-pressure warning while running | Stop safely and switch off; exact handbook instructions apply. |
| Major pedal/braking change or red brake warning | Stop safely; do not drive with unsafe braking. |
| Steering control compromised | Stop safely and seek assistance. |
| Flashing engine warning plus severe running or power loss | Stop safely and arrange assistance. |
| Significant fluid loss with a critical warning | Stop safely; no refill-and-drive assurance. |
| Smoke/burning smell indicating immediate hazard | Stop safely and seek assistance; do not approach an unsafe engine bay. |
| Loss of drive, unsafe shifting or gearbox stop warning | Stop safely and follow exact manual; no universal idle/engine-off cooling rule. |

## 17. CTA by Urgency

Routine issues may lead to an inspection booking. Safety-critical scenarios first direct the reader to stop safely and seek assistance; a workshop booking is secondary. No free check or instant diagnosis is offered.
