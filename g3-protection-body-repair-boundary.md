# G3 protection and body-repair boundary

## 1. Boundary Principles

G1 body-repair ownership is unchanged. Protecting a finish, improving surface defects and repairing lost paint are separate decisions.

## 2. PPF

Owner: `/services/paint-protection-film`. Physical film on covered panels.

## 3. Ceramic Coating

Owner: `/services/ceramic-coating`. Product-specific surface treatment.

## 4. Polishing

Owner: `/services/car-polishing-dubai`. Gloss and suitable minor surface refinement.

## 5. Paint Correction

Owner: `/services/car-polishing-dubai`. Assessed defect reduction within safe limits.

## 6. Swirl Removal

Owner: `/services/car-polishing-dubai`. Shallow marks may improve; no guaranteed removal.

## 7. Light Scratch Correction

Owner: `/services/car-polishing-dubai`. Only safely correctable surface marks.

## 8. Deep Scratch / Paint Damage

Owner: `/services/car-body-repair-dubai`. Paint depth/loss and repair scope need assessment.

## 9. Paint Repair

Owner: `/services/car-body-repair-dubai`. Repair or replace damaged paint finish.

## 10. Panel Refinishing

Owner: `/services/car-body-repair-dubai`. Refinishing is not coating or correction.

## 11. Dent Repair

Owner: `/services/car-body-repair-dubai`. Panel damage; no film/coating cure.

## 12. Collision / Body Repair

Owner: `/services/car-body-repair-dubai`. Protected G1 commercial repair task.

## 13. Paint Restoration

Owner: `/services/car-polishing-dubai`. Sound finish assessment; failed/missing paint referred to body repair.

## 14. Internal-Link Strategy

Retained correction → body-repair link; three Arabic protection pages now link to their existing localized body-repair owner where deeper damage is relevant.

## 15. Conflict Findings

Zero unresolved protection/body-repair editorial conflicts. No G1 repair page was rewritten.

## 16. Final Boundary Decisions

Inspection determines defect depth and the available scope; polishing does not fill dents, replace chips or repair failed clear coat.
