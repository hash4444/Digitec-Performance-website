version/traffic/configuration as a recovery pair. Verify the supported origin
restore operation. For the documented Lovable flow, restore that exact published

does not change those resources. Actual IDs are unknown; no executable rollback
command can safely be filled in now.

PPF, tuning, contacts, sitemap and redirects with the saved pre-release state.
Never use local source HEAD or public HTML alone as proof of complete recovery.
