# Bentley hub SEO upgrade

Target URL: `/brands/bentley-service-dubai`

## Baseline

The 2026-09-05 Search Console export records 528 impressions, no clicks and average position 48.93 for the hub. Existing child pages have stronger opportunity: transmission repair (46 impressions, position 13), suspension repair (25, 16.04) and AC repair (16, 14.81).

## Keyword ownership

The hub owns broad Bentley service, repair, workshop, specialist and service-centre intent in Dubai. Transmission repair, suspension repair, AC repair and electrical repair keep their specific commercial intent on their existing child URLs. The 200-impression reverse-camera query stays with electrical repair until demand and accepted capability justify a dedicated page.

## Content and conversion changes

- One English H1: `Bentley Service & Repair Dubai`.
- Independent Al Quoz positioning, vehicle-specific assessment and an accurate non-authorised claim.
- Internal links to every existing Bentley service URL, with focused links for transmission, suspension and AC.
- Separate model context for Continental GT/GTC, Flying Spur, Bentayga/EWB and earlier cars; hybrid high-voltage scope is not implied.
- WhatsApp and call CTAs after the hero, service section and final estimate section.
- Visible FAQs support customer questions and schema only covers visible answers.

## Technical checks

Keep the self-referencing canonical, one H1, breadcrumbs and visible FAQ markup. The route manifest updates the hub, three priority child services and two supporting guides. The sitemap is regenerated during the validation build.
