## 9. Six review-response templates

Use only when the review or job record confirms the service. Adapt to the actual feedback; do not publish vehicle registration, invoice details, phone numbers or private dispute history. Suggested internal response goal: within two working days, adjusted to staffing.

**Positive PPF:** “Thank you for trusting DIGI-TEC with your PPF installation. We’re pleased you’re happy with the finish. Please contact the workshop if you have any questions about caring for the film.”

**Positive ceramic:** “Thank you for sharing your experience. We’re glad you’re pleased with the ceramic coating and the care your car received. The team is here if you need any aftercare guidance.”

**Positive polishing/correction:** “Thank you for your feedback on the paintwork. We’re pleased the polishing and correction work made a difference for you. We appreciate you choosing DIGI-TEC.”

**General workshop:** “Thank you for taking the time to share this. We’re pleased the team explained the work clearly and looked after your vehicle. We appreciate your trust.” Use the clear-explanation sentence only if that is what the customer said.

**Neutral:** “Thank you for your feedback. We’re sorry parts of the visit did not fully meet your expectations. Please contact the workshop so we can understand what could have been better and review it with the team.”

**Negative:** “We’re sorry to hear about your concerns. We would like to review the work and understand what happened. Please contact the workshop and ask for the service manager so we can investigate this with you directly.”

If a reviewer cannot be matched, do not accuse them of being fake. Say the team would welcome enough details privately to locate the visit. Investigate first; flag only an identifiable policy violation, not an unfavorable opinion.
