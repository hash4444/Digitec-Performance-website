## Unsupported copy to remove

The common-problems guide currently says generic workshops often lack Star/software/AMG knowledge; that M276/M278/M177 pumps/thermostats/hoses fail earlier in UAE summers; AIRMATIC wears faster and certain parts are common replacements on E/S/GLE/GLS; auxiliary batteries, stabilisers and SAM are common fault points; and early diagnosis prevents major repairs / specialist repair ensures long-term savings. No workshop failure dataset or model-specific source substantiates those claims. Replace them with possible symptoms and evidence needed before parts approval. A scan is not a guarantee of preventing future mechanical failure.

The maintenance renderer's default title is acceptable but the intro asks owners to choose the best workshop, the majority of sections focus on diagnostics/repair/performance/booking, FAQs are workshop sales questions, and its Service schema describes broad commercial work. A profile-only change cannot resolve that mismatch. A Mercedes-only guide renderer/data branch should supply the planning content, three or four unique planning FAQs, related informational guides and a restrained service-hub next step. The parent has assigned these Mercedes-specific renderer edits to this subagent after the map gate.

## Official references checked

* [Mercedes-Benz Operating Fluids portal](https://operatingfluids.mercedes-benz.com/) and [engine applicability overview 223.2](https://operatingfluids.mercedes-benz.com/sheet/223.2/en): approvals must be matched to the engine/application; a viscosity or approval number is not universal. [229.5](https://operatingfluids.mercedes-benz.com/sheet/229.5/en) explicitly sends applicability to 223.2; do not recommend 229.5 for every Mercedes.
* [Mercedes S-Class 2023 owner manual: special service requirements](https://www.mercedes-benz-mena.com/iraq/en/services/manuals/s-class-saloon-2023-03-w223-mbux/assyst-plus-service-interval-display/notes-on-special-service-requirements/): difficult operating conditions/increased stress can require more frequent maintenance. This is a model-specific manual example, not a universal Dubai replacement schedule.
* [Mercedes USA service and maintenance](https://www.mbusa.com/en/owners/service-maintenance): A and B include more than an oil change, and B differs in scope. US mileage/timing examples must not be transplanted into Dubai copy. Use only as support for distinguishing scope, not regional interval advice.
* [Mercedes Dubai service/warranty products](https://www.mercedes-benz-mena.com/dubai/en/services/service-warranty-products/): package coverage is model-dependent. Coverage duration is not the service interval. Do not copy dealer package prices/benefits or imply DIGI-TEC provides an OEM warranty programme.

These sources do not substantiate workshop-specific authorization, diagnostic subscriptions, coding access, prices, turnaround guarantees or failure rates. The user has separately confirmed XENTRY availability; function/vehicle/module limits still apply.
