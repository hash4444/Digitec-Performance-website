DIGI-TEC DIAGNOSTICS & AC: EXISTING OWNER AND CAPABILITY AUDIT
19 October 2026. Read-only baseline. No application or production edits by this audit.

Evidence and limits
The structural baseline is the final prior combined release candidate dist, whose
reviewed application source corresponds to current main acef342. The immutable
selected-baseline-html directory contains all 16 selected page responses before
this sprint, with SHA256 in selected-owner-baseline.json. Metadata comparisons
should use these local baseline snapshots, not search-engine cached extracts.

The audit inspected 1,256 generated HTML routes and extracted 216 relevant routes:
12 general/Mercedes services, 194 brand diagnostic/AC/electrical routes and 10
existing Mercedes/Porsche symptom guides. Of these, 196 are indexable and in the
sitemap; 20 retained secondary-brand electrical routes are noindex and excluded.
All 216 have one H1 and a self-referencing canonical; all service owners have
Service structured data and all emitted FAQ answers exist in initial HTML.
No noindex policy change or new route is needed for the selected demand.

The separate public capture used 34 public GET requests: 32 owner locales,
sitemap.xml and robots.txt, at 2026-10-19T08:03:37.623Z (12:03 Dubai). All returned
HTTP 200. All 32 owners have one H1, one self-canonical, index/follow eligibility,
sitemap inclusion, parseable JSON-LD, crawlable FAQ answers where present and no
X-Robots-Tag noindex. This establishes crawl/indexing eligibility, not actual
Google index coverage. No Google Search Console URL Inspection was performed.

Eight strongest implementation owners (existing routes; /ar counterparts exist)
1. /services/car-diagnostics-dubai
   General diagnostic tests, fault-code scanning, check-engine/warning complaints,
   no-start investigation and electrical/mechanical triage; do not split into a
   page per symptom. The local baseline has 3 FAQs, one additional explanatory
   section, 4,950 main-content characters and 685 inbound page routes (includes
   sitewide footer links; this is not 685 independent editorial endorsements).
   Existing headline/quote CTAs and phone are available. Extend symptom/process
   detail and customer intake, while keeping coding/programming distinct.

2. /services/car-ac-repair-dubai
   General warm-air, weak cooling/airflow, leak and compressor-related searches
   belong here; condenser, evaporator and hose concerns do not justify separate
   URLs. Baseline has zero FAQ, four brief extra sections, 4,739 main-content
   characters and 31 inbound routes. No service-specific hero CTA or quotation
   guidance; generic WhatsApp/phone/end CTA exists. Highest general conversion
   improvement: add symptom-specific assessment wording and explain refill versus
   leak/fault repair and the vehicle-specific quotation process.

3. /brands/bmw-service-dubai/engine-diagnostics
   BMW diagnostic/drivetrain/check-engine/rough-running intent. 6 FAQs; 4,662
   main-content characters; 22 inbound routes. Existing ISTA+ language separates
   diagnostic scanning, coding and programming. Explain directed checks, symptom
   conditions and next-step assessment without asserting every module is covered.

4. /brands/porsche-service-dubai/engine-diagnostics
   Porsche diagnostic/check-engine/drivability intent. 6 FAQs; 4,998 main-content
   characters; 30 inbound routes. Existing PIWIS 3 claim is qualified by vehicle
   generation, module and access. Replace the inherited 9A2/battery-disconnect
   communication assumption with a symptom description if editing symptoms.

5. /services/mercedes-ac-repair-dubai
   Mercedes AC/climate-zone/cooling/leak concerns. Canonical is the /services URL,
   not /brands/mercedes-benz-service-dubai/ac-repair. 3 FAQs; 5,305 main-content
   characters; 27 inbound routes. Existing rendered English composer and Arabic
   override already use testing-first language, unlike stale raw service entries.
   Improve narrow intake/quotation depth and related diagnostic links.

6. /brands/bmw-service-dubai/ac-repair
   BMW warm-air/cooling/AC complaints. 6 FAQs; 4,933 main-content characters; 15
   inbound routes. Replace inherited symptoms that equate odour with microbial
   growth, water in footwell with a blocked drain, and assert a universal branded
   low-refrigerant cluster message or clutch operation. Similar symptoms can have
   different causes and compressor/control designs vary. Repair malformed
   refrigerant placeholder prose in the process and FAQ.

7. /brands/volkswagen-service-dubai/ac-repair
   Volkswagen/VW AC repair and cooling concerns. 6 FAQs; 5,047 main-content
   characters; 15 inbound routes. Same inherited symptom/refrigerant wording issue
   as BMW. Existing route is indexable even though Volkswagen electrical-repair
   route remains noindex. Use its diagnostic owner for EPC/warning requests.

8. /brands/range-rover-service-dubai/ac-repair
   Range Rover AC/climate-zone concerns. 6 FAQs; 5,098 main-content characters; 15
   inbound routes. Same inherited symptom/refrigerant issues. Existing owner can
   explain front/rear zone, idle/road and airflow observations without claiming
   universal JLR tools, every rear evaporator replacement or fixed time/price.

Canonical, hreflang, links and schema
All 16 selected locales are indexable, self-canonical and in the current local and
public sitemap. Each has en-AE/ar-AE reciprocal alternate references and x-default
to English. Preserve exact existing route and business/entity identifiers.
Service nodes reference https://digitecme.com/#business; global #organization,
#website and the six established business service entities remain intact.
Existing route FAQPage is emitted only from displayed FAQ data. Update real
questions/answers together; do not create invented ratings, review/AI schema or
manufacturer affiliation. The full map records headings, title/description,
schema types and IDs, inbound routes and contact destinations for each owner.

Use existing general-to-brand and brand-to-sibling links to connect a complaint
with the right owner. Brand hubs already link to their diagnostic/AC services;
do not repeat the completed five-brand optimization for more links. Relevant
diagnostic-to-electrical/mechanical/transmission paths should name the decision
they help make. Inbound counts are unique page routes, including navigation;
they do not measure link strength, traffic or GSC query-to-page ownership.

Arabic parity
Selected Arabic routes exist and are crawlable, but generic service refiners
strip extraSections and the quotation-guidance JSX is English-only. General
diagnostic Arabic metadata still advertises ECU programming/full digital scan
without the English qualifications; general AC Arabic copy is much shorter.
Target-only final overrides should preserve equivalent symptom/process/FAQ and
quotation coverage in both languages. Keep the exact route/entity identifiers.
The shared generic Arabic CTA label currently overrides service-specific labels;
ensure target-only diagnostic/AC CTA wording is used after localization.

Workshop capability
capability-evidence.json separates existing advertised claims from operational
verification. The current repository/public site states XENTRY, ISTA+, PIWIS 3
and ODIS capabilities, with conditional JLR-compatible functions. No new physical
tool inventory, licences, version coverage or technician credentials were
provided. The older Porsche audit explicitly requested PIWIS confirmation.
Do not expand tool ownership/compatibility, coding/security/key functions,
electrical module repair or hybrid/high-voltage work from this evidence.

Existing AC scope supports explaining refrigerant, leak, airflow, compressor,
condenser/evaporator and control investigations with vehicle-specific acceptance.
No new evidence establishes a particular refrigerant machine, hybrid/electric AC
oil capability, hose fabrication/replacement service or every component repair.
Explain hose/pipe leakage as a possible location, not a universal new replacement
promise. Do not invent prices, warranties, authorisation or same-day outcomes.

Mobile and conversion baseline
All selected pages have telephone/WhatsApp links and responsive CTA styles; no
dedicated mobile sticky contact bar was found. Their existing forms count is
zero; the brand-hub forms are separate preserved components. General AC lacks
an early service-specific CTA. Existing phone is +971 4 340 2223 and WhatsApp
destination is 97143402223 throughout the selected pages. Add concise intake
prompts for model/year/mileage, exact warning, when the fault occurs and recent
work; AC requests should record cooling/airflow conditions and previous recharge
or repair. Explicitly explain the WhatsApp draft must be sent and an appointment
requires workshop confirmation. This read-only baseline did not execute live
contact links or send a message. Post-change browser QA must verify 320/390px
overflow, visibility, localized labels, hrefs, draft safety and PPF/tuning/forms.

Existing Analytics.tsx records telephone_link_clicked, whatsapp_chat_opened and
related WhatsApp intent events. Keep safeWhatsAppUrl sanitization and existing
privacy/attribution conventions. A click, draft or opened chat is not a received
enquiry; qualified lead measurement requires the business's received messages,
calls or CRM evidence. No GA4 delivery or CRM receipt is established by this audit.

"Mercedes XENTRY Diagnostics Dubai | Coding | Digi-Tec", while local approved
source uses "Mercedes Diagnostics Dubai | XENTRY Fault Tracing | DIGI-TEC".
The other captured owner metadata match the approved local baseline. Raw HTML
hashes can also differ because the host transforms scripts/email markup; they
are recorded as evidence, not treated as proof of a specific source deployment.
A main-branch push alone does not prove the newer content is published.
