Safe technical explanation model
---------------------------------
Diagnostic assessment: identify the make/model/year and fitted system; record the exact warning, operating conditions and history; use compatible scan information where available; compare codes and live data with physical/electrical/mechanical evidence as appropriate; explain what is established, what needs further tests and the proposed repair. A code does not establish a failed part. A no-start complaint may describe no crank or cranking without starting; these require different investigations.

AC assessment: distinguish warm air from weak airflow, uneven cabin zones, odour/noise and loss of cooling after previous work. Similar symptoms can involve refrigerant/leak concerns, compressor or control behaviour, condenser condition/airflow, cabin blower/filter, or an evaporator/hose/pipe concern. Describe these as possible systems to investigate, not a diagnosis or a promise to replace each component. Select tests from the fitted system and evidence. Refrigerant, equipment compatibility, parts, access and accepted repair scope must be confirmed. A refill alone does not repair a leak and replacement parts should follow findings.
