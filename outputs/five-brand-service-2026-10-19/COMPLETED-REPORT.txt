DIGI-TEC five brand automotive service SEO sprint
19 October 2026 | Local review report

Local improvements are complete for the existing Rolls-Royce, Lamborghini,
Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz and BMW service hubs and their Arabic counterparts.
The changes improve maintenance guidance, supported repair scope, Arabic search
wording, internal links and the enquiry process. No new keyword pages were
created. Nothing was pushed, deployed, published or written to production.

Release recommendation: GO for the reviewed local change set. All final test
gates pass. Production release requires explicit authorization. Search growth and qualified enquiry uplift require post-release
measurement; neither is claimed from this local work.

1. Five brand audit

The production snapshot captured on 19 October shows all ten English and Arabic
hubs returning HTTP 200 with rendered HTML, one H1, self-referencing canonicals,

No X-Robots-Tag blocks were present. robots.txt permits these routes. Local
validation confirms all ten canonical URLs are in the sitemap. This establishes
technical eligibility, not Google's current indexed state or selected canonical;
those require dated GSC URL Inspection evidence.

5. Conversion changes and recommendations

Applied: consistent model/year/mileage/history/symptom checklist; confirmation
of assessment and availability after the message is received; inspection-qualified
repair estimates; parts/fluids/labour approval wording; clear WhatsApp draft and
Send instructions; expanded mobile access; correctly ordered Arabic phone digits.

The form prepares an editable WhatsApp draft. It does not persist a backend lead,
send a message or confirm an appointment. whatsapp_click, whatsapp_chat_opened
and whatsapp_draft_opened are contact-intent events, not confirmed enquiries.
Validation covers optional contact fields, meaningful issue text, error states,
brand context and exclusion of private message/query data from analytics.

Recommended next measurement: record received and qualified WhatsApp enquiries,
answered/qualified calls, and accepted backend forms separately from clicks.
Link these to appointments and service outcomes through the existing business
workflow. Header/footer placement context can be improved in a separate shared
analytics task. No backend or analytics-event semantics were changed here.

After an authorized release, compare matched complete 28-day periods and a
90-day view with consistent filters. Export query-by-page data or each exact
query's Pages report, then segment UAE and mobile. Capture URL Inspection status
and selected canonical for the ten hubs. Track qualified enquiries alongside
clicks/impressions/CTR and retain the release date.
