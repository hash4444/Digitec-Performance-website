## DIGI-TEC comparison

The [current dedicated service page](https://digitecme.com/services/mercedes-oil-change-dubai/) already provides vehicle/VIN-based oil approval and quantity selection, filter/seal scope, level/leak check, supported ASSYST reset and an itemized service record. It clearly separates oil service from complete Service A/B and fault work, links to maintenance guides and provides phone/WhatsApp booking plus an exact Al Quoz address/directions. No urgent commercial-intent rewrite is justified by this comparison.

Its parsed oil-page content emphasizes the logo and service explanation; no identifiable completed oil-service case study is exposed in that text. A future genuine workshop example, technician attribution, or approved indicative quote band could strengthen buyer confidence if the business supplies evidence. These are optional conversion improvements, not established causes of a four-impression position change. Do not invent prices, service times, oil approvals, reviews or original photographs.

## Intent ownership and action

- Mercedes oil service and Dubai oil-service variants fit the dedicated Mercedes commercial URL.
- General multi-brand scope belongs on `/services/oil-change-dubai`; explanatory comparisons belong on the blog; broader Mercedes maintenance belongs on the brand hub. Helpful cross-links are not themselves cannibalization.
- Near-me selection depends on actual searcher location and local results. Retain accurate address, map, phone and opening information; this review does not justify creating extra locality landing pages.
- Public retrieval showing both dedicated and legacy oil URLs supports checking existing redirects and Google's selected canonical. Only authenticated exact-query/page/device/country daily evidence can establish whether alternation coincides with sustained loss. No canonical ownership change or content rewrite is recommended on this evidence alone.
