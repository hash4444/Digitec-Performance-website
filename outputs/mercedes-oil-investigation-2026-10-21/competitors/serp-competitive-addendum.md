# Actual Google browser SERPs and observed organic competitors

21 October 2026; read-only. This addendum supplements the earlier web-tool comparison sample with Google's actual browser results captured by the root investigator.

## Capture conditions and limits

The browser used English/UAE search parameters and Google identified **Al Quoz, Dubai from the IP address**. All three queries have Google's personalized view and its **personalization-disabled** view. The latter is still an account-signed-in browser, not a clean anonymous session. These are desktop browser observations at one time/location; they do not establish nationwide, mobile, stable or historical ranks.

The screenshots/text also expose an owner-only Search Console Insights panel. That panel is excluded from organic order and performance conclusions. Its figures are not a live rank check or a substitute for correctly filtered GSC exports.

## Focused implications

1. These observations confirm the specialist commercial page is being displayed for all three exact typed queries in this Al Quoz browser context. They do not show a post-change trend or recovery.
2. The generic oil URL's additional appearance warrants query-by-page monitoring, not an immediate canonical transfer, redirect or rewrite. Informational blog, broad oil service and Mercedes maintenance pages can serve distinct intents.
3. The existing DIGI-TEC oil page already explains oil approval/quantity, filter/seals, supported reset, due maintenance separation and booking. A narrow future conversion enhancement could use a real documented oil job or business-approved indicative pricing; neither is a verified cause or an emergency ranking fix.
4. No backlink metrics, penalties or algorithmic explanations can be derived from these snapshots. Keep the baseline content and use authenticated GSC daily/device/page evidence to assess sustained change.
