What changed in the diagnosis
The initial export-only inspection could not establish current exact-query page ownership, UAE/device combinations or Google-selected canonical. Authenticated browser evidence now establishes all three for the current report and indexed snapshot. Historical 19 September query-labelled export covers 17 June–16 September and shows repair URL 236 impressions/1 click/23.61 position versus hub 348/0/11.84, with slash variants also present. That older overlap is superseded by the current 28-day page-owner evidence. It is useful historical context, not proof that a present duplicate or harmful cannibalization remains. Do not redirect or remove distinct oil/brake/repair owners based on query overlap alone.

Limits
No top-three result, guarantee, stable Dubai rank, causal ranking uplift, measured competitor authority score, qualified enquiry count or confirmed booking is claimed. Separate date windows overlap, and duplicate exports exist; none of their impressions/clicks are pooled. GSC average position describes recorded impressions across contexts, rather than a universal blue-link order. Different search-type labels and filters must stay visible. Missing visible rows are not zero demand. Google canonical attribution and property/page aggregation can differ.
