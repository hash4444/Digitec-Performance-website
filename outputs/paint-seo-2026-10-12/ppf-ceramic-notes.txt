Technical references
General maintenance guidance was checked against primary manufacturer sources.
These sources do not establish that DIGI-TEC installs either manufacturer's products;
no product brands, durations, specifications or warranties were added.
- Gtechniq, How to Wash a Ceramic Coated Car:
  https://gtechniq.com/how-to-wash-a-ceramic-coated-car/
  Supports careful removal of dirt and compatible washing rather than rubbing
  contamination into the paint.
- Gtechniq, How to Fully Decontaminate Your Car:
  https://www.gtechniq.co.uk/how-do-i-fully-decontaminate-my-car-to-get-the-water-beading-back/
  Supports assessing surface contamination and condition before assuming reduced
  water beading means the original coating has disappeared. The page copy draws
  only this general conclusion; no specific chemicals or reconditioning schedule.
- XPEL, Product Care Instructions:
  https://www.xpel.com/product-care
  Confirms product-dependent first-wash/curing and maintenance guidance. Fixed
  intervals were deliberately not copied into DIGI-TEC's service copy.
