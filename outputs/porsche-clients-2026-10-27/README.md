## Changes

- The English Porsche hub leads with maintenance, diagnosis and repair services, workshop photographs, cost/approval guidance and booking information.
- All 14 existing Porsche service owners remain linked. No new service URLs were created.
- Model and ownership guides are retained in a shorter directory below booking. Empty case-study promises and internal SEO language are removed.
- The hub title/description target Porsche repair and service in Al Quoz, Dubai.
- Porsche booking requests ask for vehicle details, service history, concerns and preferred timing. The page explains that the form opens a WhatsApp draft which the visitor must send.
- Macan guidance distinguishes petrol 95B coverage from the all-electric platform.
- Existing contact analytics and its privacy behavior are reused. Draft/call clicks are expressions of intent, not evidence of received enquiries or workshop visits.

## Verification

Passed app and Node TypeScript checks, focused ESLint (one existing BrandPage hook dependency warning), client production bundle and server-rendered checks. These were targeted builds, not the full site publication pipeline.

Server-rendered checks confirm one main heading, canonical URL, section order, all 14 service owners remaining indexable, and model/Arabic route rendering. Five FAQ pairs appear in schema from six visible pairs because the existing shared schema policy excludes diagnostic-tool claims. The shared policy was preserved.

English and Arabic Mercedes hub visible content and SEO match the pre-change baseline. BMW and Range Rover hub visible content and SEO also match. Pre-existing photo changes in shared files were preserved.

Interactive browser review was unavailable because the in-app browser could not access the localhost preview. No visual or interactive test is claimed.
