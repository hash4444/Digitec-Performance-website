Research date: 17 October 2026, Asia/Dubai. [authority-opportunities.csv](authority-opportunities.csv) contains eight researched opportunities with verified primary URLs, evidence/eligibility gates, priority, commercial classification and authorization status. No enquiry, pitch, listing submission, account change, review request, application, partnership or payment was performed.

The purpose is accurate discovery and useful third-party coverage. A contact page or directory's existence is not an endorsement of DIGI-TEC, proof of eligibility, a guaranteed backlink or a predicted ranking/AI citation gain.

## Priorities

1. Correct a verified existing external business record before creating another listing. The local-profile audit should establish the exact entity/name/location/telephone and identify duplicates. Do not create a second workshop or PPF profile for keyword coverage.
2. Build the first real installation evidence pack. Only then consider a factual editorial story or contribution. Editors decide whether the material serves their audience; no purchased inclusion or link requirement belongs in an earned pitch.
3. Check a manufacturer directory only after the actual installed product and current relationship are confirmed. Do not apply a manufacturer's name, badge, warranty or authorized-installer claim to the website based on the existence of its locator.
4. Treat event participation, membership, sponsored directory tiers and advertising as separate commercial decisions. Do not spend the previous paid-channel budget on an event or advertorial without a new business case and authorization.

## An evidence-supported editorial brief

The eventual brief should identify a real customer problem, the exact job and coverage/preparation decision, what was actually done, what the handover check showed and the limits. Include rights-cleared original photographs and a real workshop reviewer. Explain what the reader learns beyond a generic PPF definition. Do not offer a free installation in exchange for a link or write a third-party “review” of the business itself.

Until evidence is available, the deliverable is an internal brief and blank intake framework, not a customer case article. No pitch-ready success, certification, warranty, superior-product or volume claim has been invented here.

## Paid versus earned handling

Earned editorial means the publisher independently selects and controls coverage. Paid advertising or sponsorship must be identified as commercial; do not present it as a recommendation or demand ranking-credit links. Google distinguishes legitimate advertising/sponsorship with qualified links from links purchased to manipulate rankings. [Google link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam), [qualifying outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links).

Reject bulk citation packages, fake “top PPF workshop” awards, paid followed-link guarantees, automated comment/forum link placement and reciprocal-link pages created only for ranking. Do not request a brand partnership or membership solely to obtain a badge/link. No projected ranking, AI inclusion, lead volume or revenue is assigned to these opportunities.

## Follow-through and measurement after authorization

Keep an owner-controlled register of the verified entity, actual public source URL, action date, account/publisher owner, commercial status, permission, live mention/link, wording accuracy and corrections needed. Mark contacted, accepted and published separately. Track identifiable referral visits and real received enquiries with the existing privacy boundaries; an external mention or referral click is not a lead or booking.

Recheck a live published claim against the source before describing DIGI-TEC as featured, certified, a member or an authorized installer. Website copy, structured data and the original source must agree. Outreach and external updates require separate explicit authorization; none is part of this completed research.
