# Original PPF case-study framework and blank intake

Status: **blank internal framework; no installation case is represented here**. Prepared 17 October 2026, Asia/Dubai. Existing `verifiedProtectionProjects` remains empty. A vehicle in a workshop photograph, ceramic-coating image or body-conversion article is not proof of a PPF installation.

Use this template for whichever real completed job has the strongest evidence. G-Class, Porsche 911, Lamborghini Urus and Range Rover are candidates, not confirmed projects. Do not fill missing facts with assumptions or generate a customer article from this blank form.

## Evidence gate

| Gate | Required evidence | Pass rule | If missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real vehicle/job | Actual work-order identifier, make/model/year or generation as relevant; private record checked by authorized reviewer. | Identified real job; wording matches exact vehicle and work. | Hold project prose. Generic brand support does not qualify. |
| Installation date | Dated job/work-order/handover record; photograph metadata may corroborate but is not sole proof. | Actual installation date confirmed; distinguish photo, completion and publication dates. | Keep date pending; do not invent a date or use publication date as job date. |
| Covered scope | Actual panel list, full/partial-panel boundaries, exclusions, preparation and separate removal if any. | Customer-facing description matches completed work. | Hold coverage claims; “full body” alone is not a panel record. |
| Product/finish | Job/supply record and product document, if a brand/series/finish is to be named. | Product identification verified for this job; rights/relationship wording separately checked. | Omit unverified brand/spec/finish rather than insert a placeholder. |
| Original media | Original file and provenance; actual model, date if known, process stage and work shown. | Images genuinely document this installation and accurate captions can be written. | Do not substitute stock, AI-generated, unrelated workshop or another service's photos. |
| Publication permission | Written permission covering vehicle/images/customer identification, channels and any restrictions. | Rights and customer permission confirmed and stored privately. | Do not publish the media or identify the customer. |
| Process accuracy | Installer/workshop-reviewed description tied to recorded preparation, application and finish check. | Explain only what actually happened. | No generic process copied into a purported case as if observed. |
| Completion outcome | Dated completion/handover record and reviewer statement of what was checked/observed. | Report a specific observed outcome and limitations; no invented test or long-term protection result. | Hold success/outcome wording. A photograph alone does not prove durability, chip protection or satisfaction. |
| Publication review | Evidence references, final claim review, media redaction and approved channel/scope. | All required gates pass; responsible reviewer approves content. | Remain an unpublished brief, with reasons recorded. |

Product verification does not establish manufacturer-authorized installer status or warranty entitlement. Those require separate current documentation. No reviews, ratings, awards, prices, turnaround promises or comparative superiority are automatically authorized by a job record.

## Publication structure after all gates pass

First consider a short verified project card within the existing PPF service owner. `ProtectionProjects` already filters records by verified status and evidence. Do not set `verified: true` or add synthetic evidence strings merely to make a card appear. No new public URL is necessary for a short job summary.

If the job has enough original, useful detail for a standalone article, use this structure:

1. Verified vehicle and owner goal, without identity unless specifically permitted.
2. Starting paint condition and why the agreed scope suited this particular job.
3. Covered panels/exclusions and actual preparation/application details.
4. Original media with accurate process/edge captions.
5. What the handover check established, with limits and care instructions tied to the actual product.
6. Relevant links to the main PPF quote, the existing brand/model owner and useful comparison/preparation content.

The public title/H1 should describe the actual vehicle and actual documented task; the writer must not replace model names in a generic service-page template. A completed case does not establish a universal result for every car. Do not add Product/Offer/Review/AggregateRating markup merely because it is a case study. Any article graph must describe the actual visible article and retain the existing organization identity.

## What qualifies as original expertise

Useful case detail explains a real decision or constraint: an assessed repaint, partial-panel boundary, panel exclusion, preparation choice, complex edge or product-specific handover. It must be observed and reviewed, not inferred from generic manufacturer instructions. General technical sources can explain context but cannot certify the business's work.

The framework favors accurate first-hand evidence over mass-produced vehicle pages, consistent with [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content). It creates no claim that a case study will gain rankings, AI citations or paid installations.
