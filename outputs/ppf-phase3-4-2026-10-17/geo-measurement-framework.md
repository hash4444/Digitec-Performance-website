# Monthly PPF AI discovery measurement

Use the [monthly workbook](ppf-monthly-geo-tracker.xlsx) for repeatable observations and verified aggregate outcomes. It contains 40 planned October 2026 tests and no actual AI answers, visits, enquiries or installations. The separate [17 October baseline](ai-baseline.md) records six access attempts with zero inspectable AI answers; mention/citation status remains unknown. Four conventional search samples are separate retrieval evidence.

## Repeatable observation panel

Test the ten exact natural-language questions in [query-ownership.csv](query-ownership.csv) on Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT search and Gemini. Use one row per query/platform/run, including repeats rather than overwriting earlier evidence. The query map is an editorial ownership plan, not a result panel or search-volume dataset.

Record the actual Dubai timestamp, language, locale setting, actual testing location, signed-in state and visible model/mode. An English/UAE URL parameter or this task's Dubai timezone does not prove the network location is Dubai. Record unknown where location or model cannot be established. Keep the prompt unchanged where possible and save any exact issued variation. Near-me queries in the separate local map require real searcher-location context and cannot yield a universal ranking.

| Test status | Evidence required | Mention/citation treatment |
|---|---|---|
| planned | Test scheduled in the template but not conducted. | Unknown; excluded from rates. |
| observed | Inspectable generated answer saved with platform/date/query evidence. | Record yes/no independently for business mention and actual DIGI-TEC URL citation; retain unknown if not assessable. |
| no_ai_answer | Search/interface loaded and was inspected, but supplied no generated answer. | Separate outcome; excluded from answer rates. |
| unavailable | Access, sign-in, tool or response limitation prevented inspection. | Unknown; excluded from rates. |

A business name in text is a mention. A linked DIGI-TEC source URL is a website citation; save its actual URL and relevant answer evidence. Separate third-party mentions of the business from direct website citations. Record competitors verbatim from the actual answer and assess identity, location, service, product/warranty and quotation claims against approved records. Mark mixed/incorrect answers and preserve the exact disputed statement. Missing access is not a negative answer or an accuracy failure.

Keep evidence files free of private account/customer information. Store full answer captures only in a suitable access-controlled evidence location; use a nonpersonal pointer in the workbook. Do not infer a Google rank, comprehensive AI visibility or market share from this small panel.

## Workbook operation

1. Set Monthly summary B4 to the **first calendar day** of the reporting month; its display is month/year. Use that same real date in observation/referral month cells.
2. Complete the planned rows after a real test. Amber cells hold observation and outcome inputs; supplied query/platform/month cells may also be updated when preparing the next month. Preserve query IDs Q01–Q10.
3. Use the status and yes/no/unknown dropdowns. Save date/mode/location/evidence alongside every observation. Keep planned, unavailable and no-answer outcomes separate.
4. Copy the 40 query/platform rows for a new month. The current summary formula ranges cover observation rows 8–207 and referral rows 8–107; extend formulas, tables and validation ranges before exceeding those bounds.
5. Review answer counts and denominators before interpreting rates. Mention rate = mentions yes / observed answers with known mention yes/no. Citation rate uses its own known citation denominator. No known denominator displays n.a., never 0%.

The workbook was authored, recalculated, inspected, rendered and exported with the bundled Artifact Tool. Disposable synthetic inputs verified positive/negative rates, unavailable-result exclusion, month filtering and blank-versus-verified-zero referral behavior, then were removed. The formula scan found no errors; rendered sheets were visually checked. Native Microsoft Excel recalculation was not run. [Validation evidence](tracker-validation.json) records that boundary.

## Referral traffic and genuine commercial outcomes

The existing attribution helper recognizes controlled ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Claude source labels/referrer hosts. This is a source heuristic, not proof of a specific generated answer: an openai.com link may be documentation, and manually supplied source labels are untrusted. Missing referrers remain unavailable. Do not classify all Google organic visits as AI Overview/AI Mode traffic; Google's [AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) includes those features in overall Search Console Web reporting.

Enter identifiable monthly sessions only from an actual approved analytics extract, with date and attribution basis. The Referrals sheet's five source rows are placeholders. Blank means unavailable; enter zero only when a verified source reports zero. Label partial coverage in the evidence field. No analytics account or intake system was connected in this phase.

Received enquiries require actual intake evidence and deduplication. Completed installations require matched real job/completion records; a quotation, accepted quote or booked date alone does not establish completion. Follow the existing [Phase 2 receipt and CRM specification](../ppf-phase2-2026-10-17/lead-attribution-crm-spec.md). Reconcile the client-editable draft reference/source labels against the received message and actual records; retain unknown when no defensible match exists. Keep reporting-month/cohort conventions explicit before comparing enquiries and installations.

The website opens a WhatsApp draft and cannot confirm receipt. Page views, contact clicks, draft references, AI mentions and citations are not leads. Keep names, contact details, enquiry text, vehicle identifiers and individual revenue records out of this workbook and analytics; use approved protected operational systems and aggregate counts. No new analytics event, automatic lead conversion, messaging service or scheduled automation was added.
