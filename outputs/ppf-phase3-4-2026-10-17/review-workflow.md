# Neutral customer review workflow

Prepared: 17 October 2026, Asia/Dubai. Status: proposed process and message drafts only. No customer message, review, automation or profile edit was sent or created.

## Eligibility and timing

After an actual completed service and handover, staff may offer one neutral invitation through an appropriate customer-approved channel. Include all eligible genuine customers consistently, including customers who expressed dissatisfaction. A quotation request, WhatsApp draft or click does not establish a completed installation. Keep complaints/service recovery separate from access to the review link.

No purchases, gifts, discounts, rating incentives, employee/family impersonation, prewritten customer testimonials, satisfaction-based filtering or pressure to review on site. Do not ask for a star score, PPF keywords, a staff name or other specific review content. Google's current contribution policy prohibits incentivized reviews, selective solicitation of positive reviews and pressure/specific-content requests. [Google Maps contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en)

## Exact review destination remains pending

The genuine Google review-request URL is UNVERIFIED. A general Maps place/directions URL is not proof of the review-request destination. An authorized owner must obtain the link from the existing profile's review controls, verify it points to the correct business and record its source/date before using it. Do not construct a place ID, review URL or QR code from memory. [Google review link/QR instructions](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815?hl=en)

Until that URL and the outreach/channel permission are approved, the message remains a draft. Do not send an invitation containing a guessed or substitute URL. This task does not request additional account access or authorize outreach.

## WhatsApp follow-up copy for later approval

English:

> Thank you for choosing DIGI-TEC. If you would like to share your experience, you can leave an honest Google review using our review link. Feedback of any kind is welcome.

Arabic draft, for native owner review:

> شكراً لاختيارك ديجي-تك. إذا رغبت في مشاركة تجربتك، يمكنك كتابة مراجعة صادقة على Google عبر رابط المراجعة المعتمد. نرحب بملاحظاتك أياً كانت.

The approved actual profile-generated link may be appended only after verification. Do not add an incentive, request a positive rating or supply text for the customer to post.

## Staff steps and lightweight control record

1. Confirm the actual completed service/handover and the customer's appropriate contact permission; respect opt-outs. Do not select recipients based on anticipated sentiment or quoted value.
2. Check that the correct profile-generated review link has been verified and that the business has separately authorized the invitation method.
3. Offer the same optional invitation once. A receipt/handover QR is an alternative only when it uses the verified destination. Do not create a new messaging integration or upload contact lists for this workflow.
4. Record only the internal job reference, handover date, invitation offered/sent status, channel/permission basis and link version in the approved protected operational record. Do not put customer names, contact details, vehicle identifiers or review text into analytics.
5. Check real owner review notifications later. Reply professionally without disclosing private job/customer information; take service-resolution details to an appropriate private channel. Do not pressure customers to revise/remove a negative review, condition assistance on a rating, or ask staff to impersonate customers.

The proposed process avoids repeated reminders and review quotas. Track whether staff consistently offered the neutral invitation; never infer a review, lead or sale from an invitation click. Current Google reviews, review count, reply queue and any actual review conversion remain unverified because authenticated/profile access was unavailable.

## Release checklist

- Existing genuine profile identity and review-request link verified by authorized owner.
- Real service completion and suitable channel permission confirmed; opt-out handling documented.
- Neutral English/Arabic copy approved without incentives, keyword requests or sentiment filtering.
- Owner explicitly authorizes the outreach or handover process; no automated sending is enabled by this document.
- Public replies protect customer privacy and do not repeat unsupported product/warranty claims.

Official sources above were rechecked on 17 October 2026. That is the research/access date, not a policy publication date. The review process does not promise rankings, AI citations, new reviews or sales.
