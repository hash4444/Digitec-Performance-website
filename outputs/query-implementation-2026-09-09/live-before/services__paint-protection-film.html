    A physical layer of protection

    What Is Paint Protection Film?

    Paint protection film, or PPF, is a transparent protective film applied to painted exterior surfaces. It forms a replaceable barrier over the original paint to help reduce damage from small stone impacts, road debris and minor surface abrasion.

    Coverage matters: only the filmed areas receive that barrier. PPF does not repair existing chips or make a car damage-proof. Some films offer a self-healing surface for light marks, subject to the product’s specified conditions.

    Paint care for local driving

    Why PPF Makes Sense in Dubai

    Frequent journeys on Sheikh Zayed Road and other Dubai highways expose forward-facing panels to road debris. Sand and dust also accumulate between washes, and rubbing a dry surface can create abrasion. PPF can help reduce some of that wear on covered paint.

    Intense sun and heat make product selection and care relevant too. UV, staining and heat-related properties vary by film; ask for the selected product’s guidance. Careful washing remains essential, especially for high-value cars whose original paint you want to preserve.

    Appearance & film choice

    Clear, Matte & Satin PPF: What Changes?

    Clear gloss PPF is intended to retain the paint’s colour and glossy appearance. Matte PPF gives a flatter look, while satin PPF has a softer sheen between matte and gloss. These are finish categories, and the result depends on the underlying paint and film.

    Ask DIGI-TEC which finishes are available for your car and request a sample of the selected product. Self-healing and water-beading properties are film-specific, so confirm them alongside finish, care and any warranty terms.

    PPF Questions, Answered

    What is PPF for cars?

    Paint protection film (PPF) is a transparent protective film applied to painted exterior surfaces. It creates a replaceable physical layer that can reduce stone-chip damage and minor surface abrasion on the panels it covers.

    How much does PPF cost in Dubai?

    PPF cost depends on the car’s size and shape, covered panels, chosen film and finish, paint preparation and installation complexity. Send DIGI-TEC your brand, model, year and coverage preference for a vehicle-specific quotation. There is no single price for every car.

    Is PPF worth it in Dubai?

    PPF can be useful when preserving paint is a priority, particularly for frequent highway driving or high-value vehicles. Consider the panels exposed to road debris, your budget and how you maintain the car. Film reduces some surface damage but does not make paint damage-proof.

    How long does PPF last?

    Service life varies with the selected film, installation, exposure and care. Ask for the product’s rated lifespan, maintenance requirements and any written warranty terms in your quote. DIGI-TEC does not state a universal lifespan for all films.

    Does PPF protect against scratches?

    PPF can reduce minor abrasion on covered areas. Some films have a self-healing top surface that can reduce light marks under the manufacturer’s specified conditions. Deep scratches, cuts and damage through the film are not self-healed.

    Does PPF protect against stone chips?

    PPF provides a physical barrier that can reduce paint damage from small stone impacts on covered panels. It cannot prevent every chip: impact energy, film properties and the point of contact all matter.

    Does PPF protect against sand?

    Film can help reduce light abrasion on covered paint, but it is not sand-proof. Dust and grit still need careful removal. Avoid rubbing a dry, dusty surface and follow the film manufacturer’s washing guidance.

    Can PPF be applied to a new car?

    Yes, subject to inspection of the paint and the chosen film’s requirements. New paint can still have contamination or defects, so its condition should be checked before coverage and preparation are agreed.

    Can PPF be installed on a used car?

    Often, yes. Existing chips, scratches, previous repairs and repainted panels need assessment first. PPF does not repair damaged paint, and any necessary correction or refinishing should be discussed before installation.

    What is the difference between PPF and ceramic coating?

    PPF is a physical film that can reduce stone-chip damage and minor abrasion. Ceramic coating is a surface treatment that can improve water behaviour, cleaning and appearance depending on the product. Coating does not provide the same stone-chip barrier as film.

    Can ceramic coating be applied over PPF?

    A compatible coating may be applied over a suitable film. Confirm compatibility with both product manufacturers, including any effect on finish, care and warranty terms. DIGI-TEC can discuss combined options for compatible surfaces.

    Does PPF change the appearance of the paint?

    Clear gloss film aims to retain the paint colour and a glossy appearance, although edges and texture may be visible. Matte and satin films can change the sheen. Ask DIGI-TEC to confirm available finishes and show the selected product before choosing.

    How long does PPF installation take?

    Timing depends on coverage, vehicle shape, paint preparation and the chosen installation method. Ask for the expected workshop time and any aftercare or settling period when the team confirms your car’s scope.

    How should PPF be maintained?

    Follow the selected film manufacturer’s care instructions, including the first-wash interval and approved cleaning products. Remove dirt gently, avoid abrasive polishing and keep pressure-washer jets away from film edges. Ask the workshop about lifting edges, staining or damage.
