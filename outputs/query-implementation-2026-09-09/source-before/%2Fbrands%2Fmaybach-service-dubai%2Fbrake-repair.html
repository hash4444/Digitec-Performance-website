    When to Book Mercedes-Maybach Brake Repair

    Brake pad wear warning on the instrument cluster or head-up display
    Squeal, grinding, or metallic scrape on light braking
    Steering-wheel or pedal vibration under braking from motorway speed
    Brake fluid warning or spongy pedal feel after a long descent
    Uneven pad wear front-to-rear, common on Mercedes-Maybach platforms with a stuck EPB caliper
    Carbon-ceramic rotor edge cracks, glazing, or squeal that only ceramic-compound pads resolve

    Our Brake Repair Process

    1

    Measurement and supported scan

    Rotor, pad and caliper condition are measured. Electronic pad-wear or parking-brake data is read only where the vehicle and available diagnostic functions support it.

    2

    Confirm parts and compound

    Mercedes-Maybach pads and rotors must match the fitted Silent-tuned six-piston front calipers with ceramic-optimised pads. The proposed source, compound and compatibility are documented in the quotation before parts are ordered.

    3

    Fluid change and boil-point test

    Full brake fluid replacement (DOT 4 LV or DOT 5.1 as your platform demands) with a refractometer boil-point test before and after, critical in Dubai summer.

    4

    Bedding-in and EPB function

    The required bedding procedure, road test and any supported EPB retract or reset function are confirmed for the vehicle and included only where applicable.

    Mercedes-Maybach Parts Options, Documented

    Pads, rotors, sensors and fluid are selected for the exact VIN, fitted brake option and agreed use. The estimate may specify a genuine part, an established OE-supplier component or another suitable customer-approved option, subject to availability. Carbon-ceramic pad and rotor compatibility is confirmed before work.
