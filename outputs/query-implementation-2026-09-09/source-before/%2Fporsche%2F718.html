    Porsche model guide

    Porsche 718 Cayman & Boxster Service Dubai

    The 718 family includes mid-engine Cayman and Boxster models with turbocharged flat-four or GT-oriented flat-six powertrains, manual or PDK transmissions and variant-specific chassis equipment.

    Coverage

    Which 718 vehicles this page covers

    • 982-generation 718 Cayman and Boxster four-cylinder applications.
    • GTS 4.0, Cayman GT4 and Spyder flat-six applications where workshop scope is confirmed.
    • Manual and PDK vehicles with their distinct clutch and service needs.
    • Convertible-roof checks for Boxster and Spyder variants where relevant.

    Model-specific systems

    What changes the service and repair plan

    Powertrain architecture

    Turbocharged flat-four and naturally aspirated flat-six applications have distinct lubrication, intake, exhaust and cooling needs.

    • Identify the exact engine or drive system from VIN and build data.
    • Match oil, coolant and service procedures to the fitted powertrain.
    • Use measured evidence before naming a component as failed.

    Transmission and driveline

    Manual clutch or shift concerns and PDK behaviour require different inspection paths.

    • Record temperature, load and drive mode when the symptom occurs.
    • Confirm the transmission and fluid history before proposing service.
    • Separate gearbox behaviour from mounts, engine torque, tyres and driveline play.

    Suspension and steering

    PASM ride height and calibration can vary by variant; GT chassis parts require exact identification.

    • Confirm fitted PASM, active or air-suspension equipment.
    • Combine warning data with physical joint, bush, tyre and alignment checks.
    • Verify calibration needs after relevant work.

    Cooling and Dubai operation

    Mid-engine packaging changes access and leak visibility; front radiators still depend on clean airflow.

    • Inspect airflow, heat exchangers, fans and visible leakage.
    • Treat repeated coolant loss or temperature warnings as diagnostic concerns.
    • Use the vehicle schedule plus condition and actual duty cycle.

    Braking systems

    Base, S, GTS and GT variants can carry different steel or ceramic systems.

    • Identify steel or PCCB hardware before applying inspection criteria.
    • Measure pad, disc and tyre condition.
    • Investigate vibration through brakes, hubs, wheels and suspension.

    Electrical and diagnostic scope

    Roof, chassis, powertrain and PDK modules should be scanned without assuming one tool function fits every variant.

    • Retain warning text and event data before clearing faults.
    • Establish low-voltage health when several warnings appear.
    • Confirm supported coding, programming or service functions before booking.
