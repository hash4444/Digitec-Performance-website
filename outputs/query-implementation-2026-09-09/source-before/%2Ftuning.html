    What do Stage 1 and Stage 2 mean?

    Stage labels describe a supplier's package and vary by platform. Confirm the exact calibration, fuel requirement, hardware dependencies and testing for your vehicle. A software-focused proposal and one requiring supporting hardware have different costs and installation scope; a stage name alone does not establish suitability or a fixed power gain.

    Diagnostic scanning and module coding serve different needs from performance tuning. For warnings or configuration requests, start with Mercedes diagnostics and supported coding. Tuning availability is confirmed separately for the ECU, gearbox and vehicle specification.
