    Consultation Areas

    A VRX project starts with the vehicle

    There is no one-size-fits-all specification. The workshop reviews the vehicle and requested outcome before discussing a possible project scope.

    Vehicle condition

    The current vehicle and its condition are reviewed before a project scope is proposed.

    Intended use

    Daily use, passenger needs and project goals are discussed during the consultation.

    Interior plan

    Seating, lighting, trim and cabin requests can be reviewed for the specific vehicle.

    Exterior plan

    Bodywork, finish and exterior requests can be discussed without assuming a fixed package.

    How a VRX consultation works

    1. Share the vehicle details

    Send the model, year, current condition and the result you want to discuss.

    2. Arrange an inspection

    The workshop reviews the vehicle before confirming what work may be suitable.

    3. Confirm the scope

    Any proposed work and estimate are confirmed for that vehicle before work begins.
