    Fluid leaks and service history

    Confirm the leak source, fluid condition and applicable schedule. A fluid change is due maintenance when specified; it is not a guaranteed repair for internal wear or control faults.

    Our Transmission & Gearbox Repair Process

    1

    Identify 7G, 9G or the fitted AMG unit

    Use vehicle data to confirm the transmission variant, fluid requirement and supported diagnostic functions. AMG transmissions must not be treated as one interchangeable gearbox type.

    2

    Separate service from diagnosis

    Review compatible fault data, leaks, fluid condition and level using the required procedure. Operating checks, where appropriate, help distinguish gearbox faults from engine, mount or driveline concerns.

    3

    Agree the level of work

    Routine fluid/filter work, a control or valve-body repair, internal dismantling and unit replacement are different scopes. The estimate explains the supported option and any further investigation required.

    4

    Verify the original symptoms

    After the agreed work, check leaks, operating behaviour and relevant fault data. Adaptation or programming is included only when required and supported for the fitted unit.
