    About Digitec Performance Center

    Digi-Tec Performance Centre is an independent Dubai car workshop established in 2002. Listed service categories include vehicle inspection, maintenance, mechanical and electrical repair, body repair, paintwork, detailing and performance-project consultation.

    An appointment starts by identifying the vehicle and the concern or requested service. After inspection, the findings and proposed work can be discussed before the repair scope is agreed.

    The workshop is at Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, Dubai. To book or ask about a service, call or WhatsApp +971 4 340 2223.

    Vehicle-Specific Service Enquiries

    German & Luxury Vehicle Service

    Inspection, maintenance and repair can be requested for Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Porsche and other European or luxury vehicles, with the scope confirmed for the specific vehicle and concern.

    Chinese & Electric Vehicle Enquiries

    Owners of vehicles such as Zeekr, BYD, Hongqi, Jetour and ROX can request an inspection or service enquiry. The available scope is confirmed after the model and concern are reviewed.

    Performance Project Consultation

    Performance enquiries begin with the project goal, intended use and vehicle condition. Any proposed programming or modification scope depends on a vehicle inspection.
