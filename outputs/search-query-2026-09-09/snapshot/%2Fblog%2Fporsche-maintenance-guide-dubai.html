    Porsche Maintenance & Ownership Guides

    Practical guidance for Dubai owners. Vehicle-specific Porsche maintenance information remains the baseline; these guides explain the factors and questions around it.

    Porsche Service Intervals UAE Guide

    The baseline interval comes from the schedule for the exact model, model year, engine or electric platform and market. UAE conditions can justify closer condition checks, but they do not create one universal replacement schedule.

    Read guide

    How Often Should a Porsche Be Serviced in Dubai?

    There is no honest one-number answer for every Porsche. Follow the exact vehicle schedule, then consider use and condition when deciding whether inspection is needed sooner.

    Read guide

    Porsche Major vs Minor Service Explained

    “Major” and “minor” are workshop shorthand, not a substitute for the model-specific maintenance schedule. The correct scope is the set of items due by time, distance and recorded history.

    Read guide

    Porsche PDK Service Intervals Guide

    PDK intervals and fluid/filter requirements vary by transmission application. A 911, Macan and Panamera should not receive advice based on one generic PDK number.

    Read guide

    Porsche Oil Change Intervals UAE

    Use the time and distance interval specified for the exact engine and model year. Oil approval, level procedure and operating pattern matter as much as a headline mileage.

    Read guide

    Porsche Maintenance Cost in Dubai: Key Factors

    A responsible guide cannot publish one invented Porsche maintenance price. Cost depends on the exact vehicle, due items, condition, parts route, access, fluids and supported procedures.

    Read guide

    Porsche Battery Life in Dubai

    Battery life cannot be guaranteed by age alone. Heat, storage, short trips, electrical load, charging and battery type all affect condition.

    Read guide

    How Dubai Heat Affects Porsche Cars

    Heat increases cooling and AC demand and can expose marginal batteries, seals, hoses, tyres and fluids. It does not prove every component needs early replacement.

    Read guide

    Porsche AC Maintenance in Dubai

    AC maintenance begins with performance, airflow and leak assessment. Refrigerant should not be added automatically without confirming type, quantity and why charge was lost.

    Read guide

    Porsche Cooling System Maintenance Guide

    Cooling maintenance is model-specific because a 911, Cayenne, hybrid and Taycan can contain very different circuits. Clean airflow and leak-free operation matter, but circuit work requires the correct procedure.

    Read guide

    Porsche Brake Wear in Dubai

    Brake life depends on vehicle mass, fitted system, traffic, driving style, temperature and track use. Mileage alone cannot predict remaining pad or disc life.

    Read guide

    Porsche Tyre Wear in Dubai

    Porsche tyre wear reflects pressure, alignment, suspension, wheel condition, driving load and on AWD vehicles the relationship between all four tyres.

    Read guide

    Porsche Pre-Purchase Inspection Checklist UAE

    A Porsche PPI should identify the exact car, verify available history, scan relevant systems, inspect condition and document limitations. It cannot guarantee that no future fault will occur.

    Read guide

    Buying a Used Porsche in Dubai

    Choose the individual car, not only the badge or advertised specification. Condition, records, accident repair, warnings, tyres and deferred maintenance can outweigh a lower purchase price.

    Read guide

    Porsche Dealer vs Independent Specialist Dubai

    The right choice depends on warranty status, recall/campaign needs, required access, workshop capability, parts options and the specific job—not a blanket claim that one is always better.

    Read guide

    Porsche Warning Lights Explained

    Colour, exact message and vehicle behaviour determine urgency. A warning identifies a system condition, not automatically the component to replace.

    Read guide

    Common Porsche Problems in Dubai

    There is no responsible universal failure list for every Porsche. Owners more usefully browse by observed symptom, model and fitted system.

    Read guide

    Porsche Macan Maintenance Guide UAE

    Macan maintenance should match the exact engine, PDK, PTM equipment, suspension and history. Transfer-case or shudder symptoms should be diagnosed rather than folded into routine service.

    Read guide

    Porsche 718 Maintenance Guide UAE

    718 maintenance differs between turbocharged flat-four and GT-oriented flat-six variants and between manual and PDK vehicles.

    Read guide

    Porsche Taycan Maintenance Guide UAE

    Taycan maintenance still includes tyres, hydraulic brakes, suspension, AC/thermal systems, 12-volt supply and scheduled inspections. High-voltage work must stay within qualified, confirmed scope.
