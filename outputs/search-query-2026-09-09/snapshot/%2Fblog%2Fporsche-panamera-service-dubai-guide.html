    Porsche model guide

    Porsche Panamera Service & Repair Dubai

    Panamera generations combine front-engine packaging with PDK or generation-dependent drivetrain configurations, optional air suspension, active chassis systems and, on some variants, hybrid hardware. Accurate service planning starts with the generation and fitted powertrain.

    Model-specific systems

    What changes the service and repair plan

    Engine and hybrid thermal management

    V6, V8 and hybrid Panamera variants use different engine and cooling arrangements. A warning or leak must be assigned to the correct circuit.

    • Confirm powertrain and oil approval before service.
    • Inspect cooling airflow, pumps, valves, hoses and visible leakage using the correct system map.
    • Accept hybrid thermal or high-voltage work only where workshop scope and safety capability are confirmed.

    PDK and driveline diagnosis

    Panamera PDK generation and configuration differ from 911 applications. Shift symptoms need unit-specific data and a check of mounts, engine torque and all-wheel drive.

    • Record the exact shift, temperature and driving condition.
    • Confirm fluid, filter and service-function requirements from the fitted transmission.
    • Do not promise adaptation, coding or programming before compatible access is checked.

    Air suspension, PASM and PDCC

    A Panamera may combine pneumatic springs, adaptive damping and active anti-roll control. Each system needs separate fault identification.

    • Measure height loss and test leaks before replacing the compressor.
    • Inspect mechanical arms, bushes, joints and tyres alongside electronic data.
    • Confirm alignment and calibration needs after repair.

    Brakes and tyres

    Vehicle mass, wheel specification and fitted brake package affect wear and vibration diagnosis.

    • Identify steel versus PCCB hardware before inspection criteria are applied.
    • Check hub and wheel mounting when brake vibration is reported.
    • Investigate uneven tyre wear through pressure, alignment and suspension checks.

    AC and cabin systems

    The Panamera has a large cabin and complex climate equipment; weak cooling may be refrigerant, airflow, control or electrical rather than one assumed component.

    • Measure vent temperature and system pressures under defined conditions.
    • Confirm refrigerant from the vehicle label.
    • Check blower and flap operation where temperature differs by zone.

    Electrical and low-voltage health

    Battery condition and network voltage can influence transmission, chassis, comfort and hybrid control warnings.

    • Perform a complete module scan before clearing events.
    • Test battery condition and charging behaviour.
    • Confirm module coding or calibration support for the exact generation.
