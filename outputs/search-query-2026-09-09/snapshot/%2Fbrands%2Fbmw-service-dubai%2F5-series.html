    Model-specific systems

    What changes the service plan

    Engine and heat management

    B48, B58, N55 and N63 platforms differ in cooling circuits, lubrication and control systems.

    • Review coolant and oil consumption trends with physical leak inspection.
    • Investigate fan, thermostat, pump and airflow performance when temperature behaviour changes.
    • Confirm hybrid or 48-volt safety requirements before applicable work.

    Transmission and smoothness complaints

    A luxury-saloon vibration can come from engine operation, mounts, wheels, propshaft, differential or gearbox—not only the transmission.

    • Reproduce the symptom at known road speed, load and temperature.
    • Identify the ZF variant and fluid specification before service.
    • Use adaptations as diagnostic context, not a universal cure.

    Adaptive suspension and electronics

    Damper control, integral active steering and air-suspension equipment vary by generation and options.

    • Scan the correct chassis modules and test power supply first when warnings multiply.
    • Inspect mechanical arms, bushes and tyres alongside electronic faults.
    • Programming and coding depend on module access, software and vehicle configuration.
