    Diagnostics and warning lights

    Warning messages, misfires, starting concerns and changes in drivability are assessed with compatible fault data and appropriate physical testing. A stored code is evidence, not proof that a part has failed.

    Engine, cooling and mechanical concerns

    Oil leaks, coolant loss, overheating, unusual noise and other mechanical concerns are inspected before a repair route, parts or timing are proposed.

    Rolls-Royce mechanical repair assessment

    Transmission and gearbox assessment

    Gearbox warnings, leaks, delayed engagement and shift-quality concerns are assessed against the fitted transmission. Fluid, service functions and repair availability are confirmed for the exact vehicle.

    Rolls-Royce transmission assessment

    Air suspension and ride quality

    Ride-height warnings, leaning, compressor operation, noise and changes in ride quality require identification of the fitted suspension. Parts and supported calibration functions are confirmed before work.

    Rolls-Royce suspension assessment

    Brakes, steering and tyres

    Brake warnings, vibration, pad and disc condition, steering concerns and tyre wear are measured before parts or alignment work are recommended.

    Rolls-Royce brake inspection

    Electrical and battery concerns

    Battery drain, low-voltage warnings, no-start concerns and electrical faults are traced with appropriate testing. Coding or programming is not assumed and requires separate confirmation.

    Rolls-Royce electrical assessment

    Air conditioning and cabin climate

    Weak cooling, airflow, leaks, compressor noise and cabin-zone concerns are tested before refrigerant or parts are proposed. The vehicle label determines the correct refrigerant and service equipment.
