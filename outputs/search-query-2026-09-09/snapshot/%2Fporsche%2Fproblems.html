    Porsche Problems & Warning Signs

    Choose the symptom you have noticed. These guides explain possibilities and driving risk; they do not diagnose a vehicle remotely.

    Porsche PDK Jerking

    Repeated jerking can come from clutch control, adaptations, engine torque delivery, mounts, driveline play or internal transmission condition; one shift event does not identify the cause.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche PDK Slipping

    Slipping describes engine speed rising without matching acceleration or incomplete clutch engagement. It should be distinguished from a commanded downshift or traction intervention.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche PDK Warning Message

    A PDK warning reports a condition detected by the control system; the wording and stored event matter more than guessing a failed part.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Delayed Gear Engagement

    A delay after selecting drive or reverse may involve transmission pressure, clutch or converter control, fluid, adaptations, mounts or engine operation depending on the fitted gearbox.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Coolant Leak

    Coolant can escape from hoses, fittings, tanks, radiators, pumps, housings or other circuit components. The visible drip may be far from the highest fresh source.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Engine Overheating

    Rising temperature can reflect coolant loss, poor circulation, airflow, fan control, thermostat or engine-management issues. Repeated top-ups are not a diagnosis.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Oil Leak

    Oil may travel along covers, undertrays and airflow, so the lowest wet point rarely proves which seal or housing is leaking.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Excessive Oil Consumption

    Consumption must be measured over a known distance with the correct level procedure. External leakage and inaccurate readings should be excluded first.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Engine Misfire

    A misfire is incomplete combustion in one or more cylinders. The code identifies the detected event, not automatically a coil or spark plug.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Check Engine Light

    The warning shows that engine or emissions control detected an event. Severity depends on whether it is steady or flashing and how the vehicle behaves.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche PASM Fault

    A PASM fault can involve controlled dampers, sensors, wiring, power supply, calibration or another chassis module. It does not automatically mean the damper has failed.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche PDCC Fault

    PDCC faults differ between hydraulic and electromechanical system generations. The platform must be identified before testing.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Suspension Dropping Overnight

    A corner or axle that loses height while parked suggests air loss or control behaviour, but the leak source must be isolated before replacing parts.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Air Suspension Warning

    The warning may involve pressure, compressor temperature, a leak, valve, sensor, supply voltage or control communication.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Brake Warning Light

    Brake warnings can indicate pad wear, fluid level, parking-brake, ABS/stability or hydraulic concerns. Colour and exact wording matter.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Battery Warning

    A battery warning may concern charging, state of charge, current monitoring, wiring or another low-voltage supply issue—not just battery age.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Won't Start

    Separate no-crank, crank-no-start and powers-up-but-will-not-ready conditions. Each points to a different diagnostic path.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche AC Not Cooling

    Weak AC can result from refrigerant loss, poor condenser airflow, compressor control, sensors, valves, blower or air-distribution faults.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Steering Vibration

    Vibration may come from tyres, wheels, hubs, brakes, bearings, suspension or driveline. Speed and braking dependence help separate causes.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Macan Transfer Case Symptoms

    Low-speed shudder, binding or vibration can involve PTM/transfer hardware, but tyres, wheel circumference, PDK, mounts and engine operation must be excluded.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Cayenne Air Suspension Problems

    Cayenne height loss, slow lifting or compressor warnings need leak and pressure testing matched to the fitted generation.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche 911 Cooling Problems

    911 cooling concerns vary across 997, 991 and 992 and between Carrera, Turbo and GT engines. Front airflow, multiple circuits and leak location must be considered.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Taycan Charging Problems

    A charging failure can originate in the vehicle, cable, connector, AC/DC supply, charging station, communication or thermal conditions.

    Read diagnostic guide

    Porsche Taycan 12V Battery Problems

    The 12-volt system wakes and controls vehicle electronics; a low-voltage failure can prevent the high-voltage system from becoming ready even when the traction battery is charged.

    Read diagnostic guide
