Mercedes-Benz AIRMATIC Air Suspension

Many E-Class, GLE and GLS models run AIRMATIC air suspension instead of steel springs. The air struts and compressor are known wear points, and years of Dubai heat age the rubber bellows faster. A car that sagged overnight usually has a slow air leak, not a dead compressor, so we diagnose before replacing anything.

In Depth

Popular Mercedes-Benz Generations and Engines We Work On.

We service and repair most Mercedes-Benz models. Suspension type, brake hardware, electrics and engines change by generation, not just by badge, so we confirm exactly which platform and engine your car has before we diagnose anything or order a part. Generations covered on our Mercedes-Benz service pages include:

Generations and engines

  • C-Class: W204, W205, W206
  • E-Class: W212, W213, W214
  • GLE (ML): W166, W167
  • GL / GLS: X166, X167
  • S-Class, GLC and others: message us your exact generation on WhatsApp
  • AMG Line and lower-profile tyre set-ups
  • Engines: the four-cylinder turbo M274 family (C-Class, E-Class, GLC of the 2010s) and V6 petrol engines of the same era

What we check because it is Mercedes-Benz-specific

  • ASSYST PLUS: service is due according to how the car is driven and shows as Service A or Service B, so we read what the system actually asks for.
  • MB-Approval oil: the approval number is printed on the oil filler cap and varies by engine and emissions hardware, so we match the oil to the engine.
  • THERMATIC / THERMOTRONIC and refrigerant: we refill R134a systems only and do not refill R1234yf systems, so we check which refrigerant your car takes before touching the system and tell you before any work if we cannot refill it.
  • SAM modules and coding: a fault in one SAM can look unrelated, and new headlights, modules or batteries often need coding to the car.
  • AIRMATIC: air struts and the compressor are wear points, and alignment has to be measured at the designed ride height.
  • Electronic parking brake: the rear calipers must be put into service mode with the right diagnostic tool before the pads are changed.

Dubai factors: summer heat past 45°C, eight months of near-constant AC use, stop-start traffic, speed bumps and patched road sections. All of them wear oil, rubber bushings, seals, batteries and alignment faster than the mileage alone would suggest. We take that into account from the first diagnosis.
