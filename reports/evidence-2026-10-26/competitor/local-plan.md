Google asks businesses to use their real-world name, precise address and the smallest appropriate category set, with one profile per business. Correct business information is the objective; adding search phrases to the name is not an appropriate optimization. [Google business representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en).

Where the profile supports services, descriptions and prices can help explain genuine offerings. Field availability depends on category; do not claim the current profile lacks these before reading it. [Google services guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9455399?hl=en).

Review requests may use an official link or QR code, and review replies should be useful and relevant. [Google review-request guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en). Google's policy prohibits incentives, discouraging negative reviews, selectively requesting positive reviews, and requests for specific review content; Mercedes keywords should never be a condition or prompt for a review. [Google Maps contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en).

- GBP-reported views/search terms and interactions with the reporting window and metric definitions. Website clicks or call interactions are not verified customer calls, qualified Mercedes leads or bookings.
- Consistent local observations for the four broad Mercedes queries plus service center/maintenance, recorded at specified Dubai sampling points on mobile and desktop. Report organic URLs separately from the local pack and Maps.
- Tagged GBP website traffic if the owner approves analytics changes. Keep the website link appropriate to the multi-brand workshop; track Mercedes landing-page visits and enquiries separately.
- Qualified Mercedes enquiries, confirmed booked services and attributable revenue from actual business records. A WhatsApp click or draft opening is a micro-conversion, not a received message or booked appointment.

Local citation consistency, photos, reviews and entity markup can improve factual clarity and customer confidence. Their independent ranking contribution cannot be quantified from this audit. Proximity and competitor authority remain outside the scope of an on-page rewrite.
