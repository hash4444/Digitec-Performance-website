Built For Dubai

Airmatic, AMG & The Heat

Airmatic air suspension is a common wear point in Dubai’s heat, so we inspect it closely and repair it with quality components. On AMG cars we focus on cooling, oil and brake condition; on the EQ electric range we check the high-voltage battery and charging.

AC performance matters here more than anywhere, our AC service keeps the cabin cold through summer, and ceramic coating keeps the finish sharp.

Mercedes-Benz Service FAQs

Do you service Mercedes-Benz outside of warranty?
Yes. We handle out-of-warranty Mercedes-Benz and AMG service and repair with genuine and OEM-grade parts, keeping your car to standard without main-dealer pricing.
Do you repair Airmatic air suspension?
Yes, Airmatic faults are among the most common jobs we see, and we repair them with quality components.
Do you service AMG models?
Yes. We service the full AMG range, from a 45-series to a G 63 and AMG GT, including their major-service items.

Yes. Our technicians are equipped to service EQS, EQE and EQ-badged models’ high-voltage systems safely.
