## Days 1–14: October 26–November 8 — foundations

**Owners:** SEO analyst, frontend/hosting engineer, business owner, analytics owner.

1. Review this audit and approve a bounded implementation scope. Save current public HTTP/content snapshots and all existing redirect behavior before any later release. Keep the October 21 oil rule, other Cloudflare rules and their order unchanged.
2. Complete M04 measurement exports and URL Inspection for the hub, oil slash/slashless pair and GSC-exposed legacy aliases. Obtain latest exact 7/28/90-day windows and query × page × day/device/country data. Current separate dimensions cannot prove cannibalization.
3. Design and review M01/M02 routing corrections for legacy aliases and genuine missing URLs. Prioritize Arabic oil and body aliases observed in GSC, then the rest of the verified alias inventory. Diagnose the active hosting layer before coding a change. The healthy English oil redirect is a protected regression case.
4. Diagnose oil canonical convergence (M03), without assuming that both accessible slash forms or the recent redirect caused ranking weakness. Keep existing canonical/sitemap/internal preferences until evidence supports a separate normalization proposal.
5. Audit existing GA4/GTM event receipts and attribution, establish qualified-inquiry and booking reconciliation (M05), and request field/mobile performance evidence (M06). A WhatsApp draft is not a sent inquiry.
6. Retain the hub's working title, H1, Service A/B explanation, repair links, XENTRY description and booking controls while capturing a stable baseline. Review trust evidence and business facts before drafting claims.

**Exit criteria:** approved routing design with explicit sources/destinations and rollback; trustworthy baseline with scope limits; protected-route matrix; actual analytics access status documented. If access is unavailable, record the missing evidence and continue approved corrections supported by public evidence. Do not silently substitute sitewide device data for Mercedes data.

**Expected results:** correct routing and dependable measurement after approved implementation. No ranking jump is assumed.

**Owners:** SEO/editor, workshop technical reviewer, frontend engineer.

**Exit criteria:** each affected page retains correct canonical/indexability, useful unique intent, supported capabilities and relevant working links; event tests show consistent counting; actual Google-selected canonical is checked when available. No unresolved high-impact regression is carried into expansion.

## Days 31–60: November 25–December 24 — evidence and authority

**Owners:** workshop manager, content reviewer, local SEO owner.

- Publish only separately approved real case evidence (M13). A maintenance, diagnostic, AC, suspension or transmission job is more directly relevant to servicing than relying only on a body-conversion project. Describe actual testing, approved work and verification, with consent and no customer identifiers.
- Refine existing cost, interval and maintenance guides around their distinct questions. Preserve their owners and link to the hub/oil/service pages. Do not copy commercial booking pages into guides or assign universal Dubai service intervals.
- Verify public review excerpts and their dates, then establish a neutral review-request process for eligible customers. Do not prescribe keywords or request only positive reviews. Correct high-value inconsistent citations after verifying the true address.
- Assess legitimate existing relationships and relevant editorial/citation opportunities. Backlink quantities, DA and competitor traffic remain unknown until reliable data exists; outreach is a separate authorized activity.
- Review GLC/AMG GT service demand and existing model outcomes. Keep coverage on the hub unless recurring qualified demand, distinct repair intent, actual capability and enough original evidence justify a new page. Tuning queries do not by themselves justify repair pages.

**Exit criteria:** a small set of verified evidence assets and accurate local information, useful links and owner-reviewed claims; any new-page request includes a written incremental-demand and content case. A content quota is not an acceptance criterion.

## Days 61–90: December 25–January 23, 2027 — measurement and refinement

**Owners:** SEO analyst, analytics owner, business owner.

1. Compare full, like-for-like weekly and 28-day cohorts; review the new exact 90-day view when available. Keep Web search type, property, query classification, country/device and URL cohort stable. Record missing/anonymized queries and canonical reporting shifts.
2. Assess query clicks/CTR/position and page performance separately, with paired query-page evidence for ownership. Investigate switching only when two relevant URLs repeatedly share a target intent and harm or poor user outcomes are supported.
3. Review organic WhatsApp/contact actions, qualified Mercedes inquiries, bookings and attributable revenue. Deduplicate events and inquiries. Report lead qualification and booking rates only with matching denominators and cohorts.
4. Check whether repairs/specialist visibility was protected while service/workshop/oil improved. If a change shows a plausible material adverse effect, investigate technical and cohort differences before proposing rollback; rollback also needs the applicable approval.
5. Retain improvements supported by visibility and business outcomes. Refine weak snippets, journeys and service information selectively. Defer speculative pages or redirects.

**Exit criteria:** a 90-day review separating delivered work, verified technical outcomes, visibility trends, qualified leads and unverified hypotheses. If ranking targets are missed, identify the next evidence-led experiment; do not claim success from impression growth alone.

## Reporting cadence and decision rules

| Cadence | Review | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| After each approved release | Status/destination, content identity, canonical/robots, structured data, links, events and mobile journey | Stop promotion and investigate a confirmed regression. Existing Cloudflare rules remain protected. |
| Weekly | Seven-day query/page/device visibility; leads and releases annotated | Use as an alert. Sparse seven-day queries do not establish a durable change or cause. |
| Every 28 days | Same-cohort clicks, CTR, position, qualified leads/bookings and canonical report movement | Decide whether to retain/refine an experiment; allow recrawl/observation and avoid changing several unrelated elements at once. |
| Day 90 | Longer cohort and business review | Agree the next scope based on evidence. Top 3 remains an ambition, not a contractual outcome. |

No scheduled automation, deployment, content publication, indexing request, GBP edit or external outreach was created by this audit. This document defines a proposed cadence for the responsible team.
