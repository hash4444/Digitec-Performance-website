## Evidence-led improvements to evaluate

1. **Document a genuine relevant job.** If the business can provide a real repair or service case with approved workshop images, record the model, reported symptom, diagnostic evidence, agreed scope and verified outcome. Keep unsupported technician credentials, turnaround and success claims out. The existing G63 conversion should retain its actual context.
2. **Confirm technical and location facts.** A named responsible specialist and precise workshop/directions information can reduce uncertainty when verified. Do not change warehouse ranges or publish credentials on the strength of a competitor's presentation.
3. **Keep quotation clarity.** The current hub already explains exclusions and approval. Improve placement only if review shows that the information is difficult to reach. Publish a starting price or package only when the business confirms the applicable model, included work, quantities, VAT and validity; no invented benchmark is warranted.
4. **Use paired GSC ownership before changing targeting.** The current SERP sample supports reviewing service intent alongside repair intent; it does not justify retitling the hub or removing useful oil/service pages. Exact query × page × country × device evidence must determine whether overlap is helpful or competing.
5. **Treat canonical convergence separately.** Fresh stored reports retain oil and body slash/slashless divergence, with their slash variants indexed. Preserve this evidence and the public HTTP checks as separate sources; no slash-policy, metadata or redirect change is authorized.

These are review proposals only. No website, Cloudflare, metadata, canonical, indexing, form, message or booking action was made by this comparison.
