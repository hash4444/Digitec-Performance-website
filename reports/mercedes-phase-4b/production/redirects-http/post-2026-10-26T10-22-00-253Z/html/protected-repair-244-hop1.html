    Mercedes Service A and Service B: which work is due?

    Start with the complete service-display message, mileage and previous-work records. The model handbook, market specification and time-based requirements guide due maintenance and inspections; there is no single Dubai interval for every Mercedes.

    Mercedes Service A
    Commonly includes engine oil and filter work, fluid-level and tyre-pressure checks, brake inspection and scheduled checks. Due items are confirmed for the model, year, market specification and history before quotation.
    Mercedes Service B
    Generally adds further maintenance and inspections to the basic visit. Cabin-filter or brake-fluid work may be due under the vehicle schedule; ASSYST information and records determine the scope, rather than assuming every item is included.
    Minor and major maintenance
    These describe the scope, not a universal checklist. Additional filters, spark plugs and fluid work are listed when due for the vehicle. An oil change alone may not complete all the required service items.
    Separately quoted work
    Fault diagnosis, repairs, leaks, tyres, brakes and transmission servicing are separate unless expressly included. Additional findings are explained before extra work is approved.

    Oil, filters and vehicle-specific inspections

    Identify the engine, required oil approval, filter and fluid quantities before work. Tyres, brakes, leaks, cooling and battery condition are reviewed within the agreed inspection scope. Heat, dust, short trips and stop-start driving can justify closer condition checks; any change to maintenance follows the guidance for the exact vehicle.

    Mercedes-AMG maintenance depends on the fitted engine, brakes, transmission and use. Mention track driving or modifications when requesting an appointment. Parts, fluids and available procedures are confirmed for the model; AMG versions do not all share the same systems or service needs.

    Common Mercedes Problems We Diagnose in Dubai

    A warning message or symptom is a starting point, not a final diagnosis. These are common reasons Mercedes owners contact our Al Quoz workshop and the focused page for each concern.

    No power, no crank or the engine will not start

    A Mercedes no-start can involve the 12-volt supply, starter circuit, key authorisation, fuel, ignition, engine-speed input or another control fault. The first step is to identify whether the car has no power, will not crank or cranks without starting.

    Read the Mercedes no-start guide

    Suspension Fault or one side sitting low

    On a Mercedes fitted with AIRMATIC, a low corner or warning can involve an air spring, compressor, valve block, leak, sensor or electrical fault. Confirm the fitted system before testing; ABC and E-ACTIVE BODY CONTROL use different procedures.

    Understand the AIRMATIC warning

    Jerking, slipping or delayed gear engagement

    7G-Tronic, 9G-Tronic and AMG SpeedShift symptoms may relate to fluid condition, adaptations, sensors, a conductor plate, mechatronics or internal wear. A scan and road test help define the repair scope.

    Read the gearbox symptom guide

    Engine light, rough idle or loss of power

    The cause can sit in ignition, air metering, boost, fuel delivery, emissions or cooling—not just the component named by a stored code. XENTRY data and physical testing are used together.

    Read the check-engine guide

    Weak AC, warm air or uneven cabin cooling

    Poor cabin cooling needs airflow, refrigerant-pressure, leak and climate-control checks. A recharge is appropriate only after the cause and correct refrigerant specification are established.

    Read the weak-AC guide

    Overheating or repeated coolant loss

    A rising temperature warning concerns engine cooling. Stop safely if overheating is indicated, allow the system to cool and arrange assessment; do not open a hot coolant cap or keep driving to test the warning.

    Read the engine-overheating guide

    Battery warning or intermittent electrical faults

    A weak main or auxiliary battery, charging fault, voltage drop, wiring concern or control-module communication issue can create several warning messages at once. Any required battery registration or coding is confirmed for compatibility and quoted where supported.

    Read the battery-warning guide

    Brake warning, vibration or reduced confidence

    Mercedes and AMG brake checks cover pad and disc condition, sensors, calipers, fluid, hydraulic operation and the cause of vibration. The correct parts route depends on the fitted brake package.
