    Our Oil Change Process

    1

    Confirm the specification and due work

    Check vehicle identity, mileage, the service display and history. An oil-level or pressure warning is assessed as a fault where appropriate, rather than treated as an ordinary service booking.

    2

    Itemize the oil-service scope

    The proposed scope lists the required engine oil and quantity, filter, drain seal or related consumables, labour and applicable tax. Additional inspections or replacements are identified separately.

    3

    Carry out the agreed service

    Use the selected oil approval and correct filter and sealing parts. Existing leaks or other findings are reported before additional repair work is undertaken.

    4

    Check level, leakage and service record

    Verify the level using the vehicle procedure and inspect the serviced area. Reset the relevant service reminder only for the completed work and record the service details.

    What your oil-service quote includes

    An oil-change quotation is not a fixed Service A or Service B package. Air filters, cabin filters, spark plugs, brake fluid and transmission servicing are separate due items unless explicitly included. Use the Mercedes service hub for a full maintenance assessment.
