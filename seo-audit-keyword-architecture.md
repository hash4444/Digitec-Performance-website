# DIGI-TEC SEO audit and keyword architecture

**Audit date:** 28 September 2026. **Market:** Dubai first, UAE second. **Business:** DIGI-TEC Performance Center L.L.C., Al Quoz Industrial Area 3.

**Status: audit and proposed plan only.** No website files, URLs, metadata, content, internal links or deployments were changed. The source Excel workbook was read only. Recommendations below require a separate implementation approval.

## 1. Executive Summary

DIGI-TEC needs clearer intent ownership and measured improvement of its existing estate before it needs more URLs. The live XML sitemap contains **996 URLs: 553 English and 443 Arabic**. The site already has all 16 requested brand hubs, extensive brand-service coverage, specialist infotainment pages, and substantial Mercedes and Porsche model/problem/system content. Many examples in the brief are existing assets, not missing-page opportunities.

The workbook contains **6,789 master keywords**, but **5,910 are research or taxonomy expansions without GSC metrics**. They are useful coverage prompts, not proof of demand. Its two query sheets differ: reconciling their exact-query overlap gives **913 visible queries, 76,690 impressions and 96 clicks**, a **0.125% visible-query CTR**. These are workbook subtotals, not verified Search Console property totals.

The strongest commercial starting point is Mercedes ownership: its broad workshop/service cluster accounts for **12,059 impressions, 29 clicks and an impression-weighted query position of 16.12**. Generic oil change, battery, steering, suspension and transmission also warrant early work. Ferrari, Lamborghini, BMW, Aston Martin and Rolls-Royce should receive attention ahead of an automatic Porsche-first sequence where their specific evidence is stronger. Cadillac CUE and Mercedes COMAND/audio have unusually concrete product-system intent, but their current dedicated pages already exist.

The first technical priority is a **confirmed server/browser route mismatch**: 56 Arabic language-link destinations initially return English-homepage HTML with HTTP 200. The sampled browser journey subsequently routes to a valid Arabic brand hub. Unknown routes initially return the homepage, then display a 404/noindex screen after JavaScript runs. This is a narrow P0 response-integrity recommendation, not a claim that the whole site is blocked or that Google has indexed those fallback URLs.

The principal consolidation candidates are generic workshop/near-me landing pages and several `best-*-workshop` pages that substantially repeat brand-hub commercial intent. This audit identifies **intent overlap**, not proven ranking cannibalization: neither workbook query sheet provides landing pages, and no query-by-page history is supplied. Owner selection must be validated before redirects.

**Recommended first implementation release: zero new URLs.** Establish the measurement baseline, decide the clearest overlap cases, then improve a small Mercedes group and selected generic services. Keep model and new-system expansion behind evidence and content-quality gates. Preserve existing useful pages while evaluating them; do not bulk-delete the large site estate.

## 2. Current Website Architecture

The current structure already combines `/brands/`, `/services/`, `/blog/`, `/mercedes/`, `/porsche/` and Arabic `/ar/` equivalents. Navigation exposes Services, Brands, tuning/GAD, VRX, Blog and FAQ. The footer links major generic services and the Mercedes hub. The home page also links directly to CUE, head-unit repair, audio upgrades and paint-care services. See the [home page](https://digitecme.com/) and [service directory](https://digitecme.com/services).

| Existing family | Observed role | Architecture decision |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Business identity, independent workshop, Al Quoz location, general service discovery | Own generic workshop/garage/location intent; retain one physical-location story |
| `/brands` and `/brands/{brand}-service-dubai` | Brand discovery and commercial repair/service hubs | Preserve current hubs; consolidate synonymous workshop/specialist intent here |
| `/brands/{brand}-service-dubai/{service}` | Brand-specific repair and maintenance | Improve selected existing pages; do not replicate a uniform grid across every brand |
| `/services/mercedes-…-dubai` | Established Mercedes service owners | Keep these URLs; do not migrate merely to match other brands' folders |
| `/services/{service}` | Generic commercial services and selected specialist services | Distinguish broad hubs, specific repair jobs, upgrades and comparison pages |
| `/mercedes/problems/…`, Mercedes model URLs, selected legacy `/blog/…` model URLs | Problem explanations and model support | Maintain a commercial owner for each intent; review overlapping guide pairs |
| `/porsche/systems/…`, `/porsche/problems/…`, `/porsche/guides/…`, model/generation URLs | Extensive supporting topical architecture | Improve navigation and quality before expanding further |
| `/blog/…` | Maintenance guides, symptoms, workshop selection and case studies | Keep informational tasks distinct from transactional service pages |
| `/best-*-workshop-dubai` | Additional workshop landing/comparison pages | Evaluate individually for consolidation; several repeat hub intent |
| `/ar/…` | Arabic equivalents and some intentionally limited coverage | Preserve genuine language alternatives; review Arabic counterparts when consolidating English |

The sitemap contains **38 English brand hubs and 310 nested brand descendants**, broader than this brief's 16-brand focus. Other brands already on the site are not automatically irrelevant or removal candidates. Existing BMW models, Ferrari models, Mercedes models and Porsche guides make blanket model-page creation especially inappropriate.

Detailed HTTP/metadata, headings, canonical, schema, word-count and link evidence is retained with the crawl. Word counts are screening indicators rather than quality or ranking thresholds. A short useful location page can be valid; a longer interchangeable service template can still lack distinctive value.

The important-page appendix before the final master table gives 33 individual audits, including all 16 target hubs. The full inventory retains literal meta descriptions, heading structures, links and schema for every fetched URL. The brand/system review and its primary-source references are also retained in [the intent evidence](audit-evidence/intent/intent-brand-review.md).

## 4. Keyword Universe Findings

All seven worksheets were inspected: README, Master Keywords, GSC Opportunities, Brand Opportunity, Service Taxonomy, Brand Specific Systems and Page Strategy. The 182 taxonomy rows (181 unique terms) and 94 brand-system rows support topical review, while the six Page Strategy rules are recommendations to audit rather than instructions to execute.

| Master evidence category | Rows | Appropriate use |
|---|---:|---|
| Existing GSC queries | 879 | Measured query evidence, subject to provenance limits |
| Generated taxonomy | 5,779 | Potential sections, FAQs and coverage ideas |
| Brand-specific research | 131 | Validate system/vehicle applicability and demand |
| Total | **6,789** | Keyword universe, not a URL production quota |

The existing P1/P2/P3 labels are not a sufficient prioritization model. README calls any observed query with at least 100 impressions P1 and can call generated commercial terms P2. That conflates measured demand with generated plausibility and ignores existing coverage, ambiguity and implementation risk.

Specific corrections:

- There are no exact duplicate keywords within either main query sheet. Two Master pairs differ only by invisible U+200B spaces: rows 138/472 and 190/503. Their metrics differ, so retain raw records until the original export can be checked, while consolidating their content intent. Rows 44/395 add a Mercedes-Benz hyphen variant. These are real duplicate/near-duplicate findings, not hypothetical normalization rules.
- `wheel alignment` repeats at Service Taxonomy B82 and B168 under Steering and Tyres & wheels. Select the actual alignment/tyre service owner and cross-link from steering; do not create a second alignment page for the alternate taxonomy label.
- All Master rows have just two intent labels, local transactional or commercial investigation. `car battery life in uae` (Master row 609; 29 impressions, position 8.07) is informational. `car body repair history dubai` (row 744; 23 impressions) needs a vehicle-history interpretation. Also, 651 of 879 observed queries sit in an unhelpful “Observed GSC long-tail” catch-all. Reclassify before briefing content.
- Six generic brake/transmission/suspension queries were assigned to Aston Martin in Master, representing **1,144 impressions**. The Opportunities sheet correctly treats them as generic. `air suspension repair` was assigned to Rolls-Royce in Master despite **106 generic impressions**. Brand Opportunity does not justify copying these Master errors into page planning.
- `cue screen replacement in dubai` belongs provisionally with Cadillac CUE. `command unit repairing dubai` is a plausible COMAND misspelling, not proof of generic engine/ECU repair demand. Validate the actual query landing page before final attribution.
- `volkswagen dsg oil change` belongs with DSG/transmission maintenance, not engine-oil change. `porsche turbo repair/service` can refer to a component or a Turbo model designation; do not automatically produce a turbocharger page.
- Repair/repairs, service/servicing, workshop/garage/specialist, gearbox/transmission, tyre/tire, screen/touchscreen, and relevant brand spellings should be normalized for grouping. Preserve original query strings and metrics for traceability.
- Dubai, UAE, Al Quoz, “near me” and “nearby” normally modify the same service intent. Their existence does not justify separate location URLs. “Near me” should be satisfied through actual address, directions and business relevance.
- Component symptoms need semantic review: a black screen may concern infotainment, a camera or an instrument cluster; shaking can come from ignition, tyres, brakes or driveline. Keyword matching alone cannot select a repair page safely.
- `authorized`, dealer and service-center queries must not lead to unsupported affiliation claims. An independent workshop can explain its status and scope clearly.
- Non-target brands are outside this expansion brief, not necessarily irrelevant to the operating business. Artarmon is outside the target geography. Abu Dhabi intent needs regional-service relevance, not a fabricated branch.

The audit's **148 analytical GSC buckets** are a reconciliation aid. Multiple buckets can share one URL as sections; informational and commercial variants can be separated only when the user's task warrants it. The final architecture must be much smaller than a brand × service × symptom × model matrix.

## 22. Priority Score Methodology

The score is a transparent planning heuristic from 0–100. It measures a recommendation's relative opportunity under present evidence, not search volume, keyword difficulty, expected leads or a promise of ranking improvement. Unknown commercial margins, backlink authority and competitive strength are not invented.

The accompanying priority ledger records each scored row's evidence and assigned factors. Query cluster impressions are counted once in the analytical source; rows that reuse a cluster for a subtopic are explicitly marked “subset/parent evidence” and must not be summed.

| Factor | Maximum | Rule / evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Observed demand | 20 | `20 × min(1, log10(1 + impressions) / 4)`; research-only = 0 |
| Ranking opportunity | 15 | Weighted position >3–10: 13; >10–20: 15; >20–30: 12; >30–50: 6; >50: 2; ≤3: 3; unknown: 0 |
| CTR headroom | 10 | Measured CTR <0.5%: 10 if position ≤30, otherwise 5; 0.5–<2%: 5 if ≤30, otherwise 2; ≥2% or unknown: 0. These are internal triage cutoffs, not industry CTR benchmarks |
| Commercial intent | 10 | Direct service/workshop 10; mixed comparison 7; informational 3; irrelevant 0 |
| Revenue potential | 10 | Relative job-value judgment: complex repair/upgrade 9, workshop/service 8, routine small job 6; no actual margin claim |
| Brand importance | 5 | Brief's target brands and core generic work 5; supported outside-scope preservation 2; unsupported 0 |
| Service importance | 5 | Core repair/safety/diagnostics 5; protection/upgrade 4; support content 2 |
| Existing authority | 5 | Neutral 2.5 unless page-specific historical evidence is available; a query impression is not proof of a URL's authority |
| Coverage gap | 10 | Strong missing/contradictory coverage 8; specific improvement/section 5; established suitable coverage 2; unknown 0 |
| Competition / difficulty | 5 | Neutral 2.5 without a proper localized competitor/backlink study; not a fabricated difficulty score |
| Likelihood of practical improvement | 5 | Clear existing-owner improvement 4; conditional section/limited evidence 3; preservation/monitoring 2; speculative new page 1 |

Round the sum to a whole number. **P1 ≥75; P2 60–74; P3 40–59; P4 <40.** Irrelevant/unsupported intent is capped at P4 regardless of impressions. Low-evidence research proposals can remain MONITOR even if commercial relevance raises their score. P0 is a separate override for a confirmed critical accessibility, routing/status, indexing or canonical defect; it is not awarded simply because a task is in Batch 0. Technical/consolidation risks are reviewed separately and labeled with their rationale rather than disguised as keyword metrics.

Non-keyword overrides are explicit: **95** for the confirmed server/client response defect, **80** for missing measurement prerequisites, **78** for directly observed commercial-owner consolidation candidates, and **65** for the specific short-guide/template review risks. These are analyst urgency judgments, not values calculated from invented impressions. Ambiguous industrial intent is capped at **20** and irrelevant geography at **0**. The override rationale and the unmodified component inputs are both in the ledger. At 100 impressions the demand component is about 10; at 1,000 it is about 15; at 10,000 it is capped at 20.

## 26. Risks / Things We Should NOT Do

- Do not turn 6,789 keywords into pages or treat generated keywords as measured search demand.
- Do not rebuild the entire architecture: the site already has hundreds of indexable URLs and substantial model/system coverage.
- Do not claim confirmed cannibalization or unintended ranking URLs without query-page data.
- Do not redirect distinct commercial, informational, comparison and upgrade tasks merely because their wording overlaps.
- Do not publish neighborhood branches, “near me” variants, empty model/year pages or unsupported dealer claims.
- Do not replace uncertainty with fabricated search volumes, margins, difficulty scores, lead forecasts or technical capability.
- Do not give every brand the same gearbox, suspension, diagnostic or electrification description. Verify exact vehicle applicability.
- Do not mistake a search-tool cache miss or a crawler-UA challenge for a site outage or Googlebot block.
- Do not assume `index,follow` and a self-canonical mean Google indexed the page; do not assume a sitemap omission means a URL is missing.
- Do not use word count, FAQ schema or a long list of system names as a quality proxy. FAQs have user value without a rich-result promise.
- Do not rename established blog/model URLs for tidiness, globally canonicalize Arabic to English, or remove useful non-target-brand pages solely to shrink counts.
- Do not implement any part of this plan during the audit. The report and supporting evidence are the deliverables; implementation remains a separate approval decision.
